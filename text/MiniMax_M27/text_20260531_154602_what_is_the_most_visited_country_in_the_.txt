According to the latest data from the UN World Tourism Organization (UNWTO), **France** is the most visited country in the world. In recent years, it has consistently topped global tourism rankings, welcoming over 80–100 million international visitors annually.

Here are some key points:
- **France** leads due to its mix of culture, history, cuisine, and iconic landmarks like the Eiffel Tower, French Riviera, and Loire Valley.
- **Spain** and the **United States** often rank second and third, respectively, in terms of international arrivals.
- The ranking can vary slightly depending on whether you measure by *arrivals*, *tourism revenue*, or *overnight stays*.

So if you’re planning a trip or just curious, France remains the top destination globally! 🌍✈️
