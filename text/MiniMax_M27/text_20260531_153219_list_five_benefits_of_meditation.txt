**Five Benefits of Meditation**

1. **Stress Reduction** – Regular meditation lowers cortisol levels and helps the body relax, reducing the physical and emotional impact of everyday stressors.  
2. **Improved Focus & Concentration** – Practices such as mindfulness train the mind to stay present, enhancing attention span and mental clarity.  
3. **Emotional Well‑being** – Meditation encourages a non‑reactive awareness of thoughts and feelings, which can lead to greater resilience, lower anxiety, and a more positive mood.  
4. **Better Sleep** – By promoting relaxation and reducing mental chatter, meditation can help you fall asleep faster and enjoy deeper, more restorative sleep.  
5. **Physical Health Benefits** – Studies show that meditation can lower blood pressure, support immune function, and even reduce markers of inflammation in the body.
