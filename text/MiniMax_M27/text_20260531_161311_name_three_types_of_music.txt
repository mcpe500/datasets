Sure! Here are three well‑known types (or genres) of music:

1. **Classical** – orchestral and chamber music from the Baroque, Classical, and Romantic periods (e.g., Beethoven, Mozart, Bach).  
2. **Jazz** – improvisational music rooted in African‑American traditions, featuring swing, bebop, cool jazz, etc. (e.g., Miles Davis, John Coltrane).  
3. **Hip‑Hop/Rap** – rhythm‑driven music centered on rapped vocals, beats, and sampling, originating in the Bronx in the 1970s (e.g., Kendrick Lamar, Jay‑Z).
