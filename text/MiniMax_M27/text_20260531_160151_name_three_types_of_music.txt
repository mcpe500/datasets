Here are three well‑known types of music:

1. **Classical** – Orchestral and chamber music from the Western art‑music tradition (e.g., symphonies, sonatas, operas).
2. **Jazz** – A genre rooted in African‑American communities that emphasizes improvisation, syncopation, and complex harmonies (e.g., bebop, swing, cool jazz).
3. **Rock** – A broad genre that grew out of rock‑and‑roll in the 1950s, featuring electric guitars, strong rhythms, and often lyrics that address social or personal themes (e.g., classic rock, alternative rock, punk).
