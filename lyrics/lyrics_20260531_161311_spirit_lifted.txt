**Verse 1**  
Morning light breaks through the night,  
my heart begins to take its flight.  
All the doubts begin to fade,  
when the spirit lifts, I’m made.  

**Verse 2**  
Storms may roar and shadows loom,  
yet a hopeful tune drums in my room.  
Each step forward feels like a sway,  
my soul ignites, I find my way.  

**Verse 3**  
Friends stand close, their smiles a glow,  
their gentle hands help me to grow.  
Together we lift the heavy load,  
and share the light along the road.  

**Verse 4**  
When the world feels heavy and gray,  
I hear the sunrise whisper, “Stay.”  
With every breath I rise anew,  
spirit lifted, I’m shining through.  

**Chorus**  
Spirit lifted, we rise on the wind,  
together we climb, together we ascend.  
When the night seems endless, we share the light,  
spirit lifted, we dance through the night.
