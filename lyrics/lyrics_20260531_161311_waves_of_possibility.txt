**Verse 1**  
The sunrise paints the horizon gold,  
A tide rolls in, a story untold.  
Each swell lifts a whispered dream,  
A promise that the future can gleam.  

**Verse 2**  
Beneath the foam, a quiet roar,  
Invites the heart to explore.  
From the shore we step into the deep,  
Where every crest is a promise to keep.  

**Verse 3**  
The wind that hums across the bay,  
Stirs the mind in a gentle sway.  
We ride the rhythm, we chart the course,  
Turning chance into purposeful force.  

**Verse 4**  
At dusk the ocean whispers low,  
“Your possibilities ebb and flow.”  
So we dive, we surf, we rise again,  
Turning the tide into our nearest friend.  

**Chorus**  
Waves of possibility, rolling free,  
Pulling us forward, wild and boundless sea.  
Ride the crest, let the horizon call,  
Together we rise—ever‑rising, ever tall.
