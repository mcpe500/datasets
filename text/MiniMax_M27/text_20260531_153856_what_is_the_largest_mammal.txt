The **blue whale (Balaenoptera musculus)** is the largest mammal—and indeed the largest animal—known to exist.  

- **Length:** up to about 30 m (roughly 100 ft).  
- **Weight:** up to around 199 metric tons (≈ 220 short tons).  
- **Heart size:** about the size of a small car.  

Blue whales are found in all oceans, primarily in sub‑polar waters, and they feed mainly on krill. While other whales (e.g., fin whales) are also huge, the blue whale holds the record for both length and mass. If you’re curious about the largest **land** mammal, that’s the African elephant.
