Here are a few playful tongue‑twisters that all feature a tiger (and a whole lot of “t”s and “r”s to make them tricky):

- **Tight tiger ties tiny tangled tassels.**  
- **Terrified tiger trembles, tugging trembling toads.**  
- **Ruthless tiger roars, rattling rusty rails.**  
- **Tricky tiger tackles twelve tangled turtles.**  
- **The tawny tiger tight‑roped through the thick, thorny thicket.**  

Give any of them a try—you’ll be tripping over those repeated sounds in no time!
