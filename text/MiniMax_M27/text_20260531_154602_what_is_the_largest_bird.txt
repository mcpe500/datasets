The **ostrich (Struthio camelus)** is the largest living bird. It can stand up to 2.8 m (9 ft) tall and weigh as much as 156 kg (345 lb).  

If you include extinct species, the **elephant birds** (Aepyornis) of Madagascar were even heavier—some estimates put them at around 500 kg (1,100 lb)—making them the biggest birds known to have existed. But among birds alive today, the ostrich holds the record.
