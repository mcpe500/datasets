The **Pacific Ocean** is the largest body of water on Earth.

| Feature | Value |
|---------|-------|
| **Area** | ≈ 165 million km² (≈ 63 million mi²) |
| **Volume** | ≈ 710 million km³ (≈ 170 million mi³) |
| **Average depth** | ~4,000 m (≈ 13,100 ft) |
| **Deepest point** | Mariana Trench – about 11,000 m (≈ 36,000 ft) below the surface |
| **Location** | Stretches from the western coast of the Americas to the eastern coast of Asia and Australia |

It accounts for roughly **30 %** of the planet’s total surface area and is larger than all the land masses combined. The next‑largest oceans (Atlantic, Indian, Southern, and Arctic) are considerably smaller.
