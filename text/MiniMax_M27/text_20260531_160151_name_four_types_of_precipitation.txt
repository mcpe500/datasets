The four main categories of precipitation are:

1. **Rain** – liquid water droplets that fall from clouds.
2. **Snow** – ice crystals that form and fall as frozen flakes.
3. **Sleet (or ice pellets)** – small, translucent balls of ice that result from freezing rain or partially melted snow refreezing.
4. **Hail** – lumps or chunks of ice that grow as they are lifted and cooled repeatedly inside a thunderstorm.
