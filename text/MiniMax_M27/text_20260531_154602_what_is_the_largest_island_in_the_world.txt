The largest island in the world is **Greenland**.  

- **Area**: Approximately 2,166,086 km² (836,331 sq mi).  
- **Location**: Situated between the North Atlantic and Arctic Oceans, east of Canada and west of Denmark (it is an autonomous territory within the Kingdom of Denmark).  
- **Why it’s an island, not a continent**: Although it’s huge, Greenland’s landmass is smaller than the smallest continent (Australia), and it sits on a continental plate rather than being a separate landmass that would be classified as a continent.  

All other major islands—such as New Guinea, Borneo, and Madagascar—are significantly smaller.
