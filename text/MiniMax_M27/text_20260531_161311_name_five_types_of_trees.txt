Here are five common types of trees:

1. **Oak** – a deciduous tree known for its strong wood and lobed leaves.
2. **Pine** – an evergreen conifer with needle‑like leaves and woody cones.
3. **Maple** – a deciduous tree famous for its serrated, palm‑shaped leaves and vibrant autumn colors.
4. **Birch** – a deciduous tree recognized for its white bark and small, triangular leaves.
5. **Spruce** – an evergreen conifer with short, sharp needles and dense, conical shape.
