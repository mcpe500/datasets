The most abundant gas in Earth’s atmosphere is **nitrogen (N₂)**, which makes up about **78 %** of the dry air by volume.  

For reference, the major components of the atmosphere are roughly:

| Gas | Approximate Volume % |
|-----|----------------------|
| Nitrogen (N₂) | ~78 % |
| Oxygen (O₂)   | ~20.9 % |
| Argon (Ar)    | ~0.93 % |
| Carbon dioxide (CO₂) | ~0.04 % (and rising) |
| Trace gases (e.g., neon, helium, methane, ozone) | <0.01 % combined |

Water vapor can vary from 0 to about 4 % depending on humidity, but when averaged over the whole atmosphere the dry‑air composition above is used.
