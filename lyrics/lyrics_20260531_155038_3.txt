**Verse 1**  
Cobblestones shimmer beneath a silver sky,  
Your laugh still echoes in the quiet lane.  
The scent of rain on warm pavement floats by,  
A snapshot of us, forever to remain.  

**Verse 2**  
Faded photographs curl at the edges of time,  
Sun‑kissed smiles linger in amber light.  
We chased the sunrise with carefree rhyme,  
Now the porch sits still, dust‑kissed but bright.  

**Verse 3**  
Midnight melodies rise from an old piano,  
Each key a word in a story we told.  
The streets where we walked still whisper “amano”,  
A rhythm that guides us, gentle and bold.  

**Verse 4**  
Sunsets bleed orange over the city’s hum,  
And the echo of footsteps fades in the haze.  
Yet in the silence, the stars hum a sum,  
Of moments we cherish, of love that stays.  

**Chorus**  
Memories linger like a sweet refrain,  
Nostalgia weaves a soft, timeless song.  
We hold each fragment, the loss and the gain,  
And let the past keep our hearts ever strong.
