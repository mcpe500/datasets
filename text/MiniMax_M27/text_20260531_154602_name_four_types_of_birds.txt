Here are four common types of birds:

- **Eagle** – a large bird of prey known for its keen eyesight and powerful flight.  
- **Sparrow** – a small, active songbird often seen in urban and rural areas.  
- **Penguin** – a flightless seabird adapted to life in cold, Southern‑hemisphere waters.  
- **Owl** – a nocturnal bird of prey with excellent night vision and silent flight.
