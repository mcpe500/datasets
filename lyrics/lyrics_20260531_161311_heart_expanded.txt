**Heart Expanded**  

**Verse 1**  
When silence fell and doubts drew near,  
I let the breath of night appear,  
A quiet ember lit inside—  
My chest unfurled, no walls to hide.  

**Chorus**  
Heart expanded, boundless sky,  
Every beat a sunrise high.  
Love runs deeper than the sea,  
Open wide—eternally.  

**Verse 2**  
Your laughter fell like summer rain,  
I caught each drop, let love remain.  
The boundaries that once stood tall,  
Now melt like snow at spring’s first call.  

**Chorus**  
Heart expanded, boundless sky,  
Every beat a sunrise high.  
Love runs deeper than the sea,  
Open wide—eternally.  

**Verse 3**  
In strangers’ eyes I see the flame,  
Compassion flows, I stake no claim.  
Each gentle touch expands the pulse,  
A chorus sung without a hush.  

**Chorus**  
Heart expanded, boundless sky,  
Every beat a sunrise high.  
Love runs deeper than the sea,  
Open wide—eternally.  

**Verse 4**  
Now the world feels wide and warm,  
A garden grows within my form.  
Infinite rhythm, steady flow,  
My heart keeps beating—ever‑grow.  

**Final Chorus**  
Heart expanded, boundless sky,  
Every beat a sunrise high.  
Love runs deeper than the sea,  
Open wide—eternally.
