The **Pacific Ocean** is the largest ocean on Earth.  

- **Area:** about 165 million km² (≈63 million sq mi) – roughly 30 % of the planet’s surface.  
- **Volume:** roughly 710 million km³ – the greatest volume of any ocean.  
- **Location:** lies between the western coasts of the Americas and the eastern coasts of Asia and Australia.  

It’s followed in size by the Atlantic Ocean, Indian Ocean, Southern (Antarctic) Ocean, and Arctic Ocean.
