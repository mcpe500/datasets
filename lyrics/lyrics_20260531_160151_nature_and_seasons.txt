**Verse 1 – Spring**  
Cherry blossoms whisper in the morning rain,  
Birdsong lifts the dew‑kissed fields awake.  
Tender shoots push through the earth again,  
And every blossom sings a bright new day.  

**Chorus**  
Seasons turn, we dance and breathe,  
Nature’s song beneath the trees.  
From bloom to frost we sway and soar,  
In every change we find our core.  

**Verse 2 – Summer**  
Golden sunlit rivers glide through meadow’s sigh,

Fireflies spark the dusk as night draws nigh,  
And summer hums a warm, sweet chorus.  

**Chorus**  
Seasons turn, we dance and breathe,

**Verse 3 – Autumn**  
Crimson leaves pirouette on a crisp, cool wind,

**Verse 4 – Winter**  
Frost drapes the hills in silver lace,  
Quiet woods hold a hushed, snowy hush.  
Snowflakes drift like whispered, fleeting grace,  
Winter glimmers in a crystal hush.  

**Chorus**
