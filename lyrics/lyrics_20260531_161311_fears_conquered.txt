**Title: “Brave Heart”**  

**Verse 1**  
The night was dark, the whispers sharp,  
My shadow danced where courage sparred.  
I shook and stumbled, heart in chains,  
But whispered, “Face the roar, don’t wane.”  

**Verse 2**  
I lit a spark from trembling hands,  
And let it grow through shattered bands.  
Each step I took turned fire to light,  
The trembling turned to steady flight.  

**Verse 3**  
I met the ghosts that made me freeze,  
And stared them down on bended knees.  
Their hiss dissolved to gentle sighs,  
As hope re‑wrapped my once‑cold eyes.  

**Verse 4**  
Now standing tall, the world feels wide,  
The fear‑filled storms have left the tide.  
I wear my scars like medals bright,  
A living proof that I can fight.  

**Chorus**  
We conquer fear, we rise and shine,  
No longer bound by doubt’s design.  
With every breath we own the night,  
Our hearts are free, our souls take flight.
