**Verse 1**  
A sunset slips through the hourglass, gold on the wall,  
I chase the echo of your laugh, a whisper that won’t fall.  
Seconds pile like autumn leaves, each one a fragile sign,  
And the past whispers, “Hold on tight,” as the present folds into time.  

**Verse 2**  
The clock’s soft tick becomes a drum, a rhythm in my chest,  
Memories are old vinyl spins, replayed with every guess.  
I trace the lines upon my palms, the map of days gone by,  
Each scar a story, each bright flash a promise in the sky.  

**Verse 3**  
Morning light drapes over coffee stains, a canvas stained with hue,  
The scent of rain on ancient streets still pulls my thoughts from you.  
Moments linger like a photograph, faded yet still clear,  
Time may steal the colors, but the feeling lingers here.  

**Verse 4**  
When nightfall folds the world in black, the stars become my guide,  
I count the years like beads on string, each one a treasured tide.  
The past and future dance in circles, never truly apart,  
And in the heart’s quiet chamber, memory becomes the art.  

**Chorus**  
Time will turn, but memory stays, a song that never ends,  
A melody that binds the days, and writes on us what it sends.  
Hold it close, let it flow, through every breath we spend,  
‘Cause in the echo of a moment, we forever blend.
