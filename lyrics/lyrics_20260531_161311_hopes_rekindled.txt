**Rekindled Hope**

**Verse 1**  
The night had swallowed every star,  
But I kept a candle hidden in my heart.  
A whisper rose, a faint warm glow—  
A promise that the darkness wouldn't last.  

**Verse 2**  
Through broken roads and shattered dreams,  
I found a spark where nothing seemed.  
It grew with every breath I took,  
A fire that refused to be consumed.  

**Verse 3**  
The wind tried hard to snuff it out,  
Yet each defeat just lit a brighter route.  
I learned that hope, like embered coal,  
Grows stronger when you let it burn.  

**Verse 4**  
Now sunrise paints the sky in gold,  
And stories left are brave and bold.  
I walk ahead with steps of flame—  
Rekindled hopes will never fade.  

**Chorus**  
Hopes rekindled, brighter than before,  
We rise again, we find new doors.  
In every heart a fire ignites,  
Guiding us through endless nights.
