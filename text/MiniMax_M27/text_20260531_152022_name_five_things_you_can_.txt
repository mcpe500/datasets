Sure! Here are five things you might see when you look up at the sky:

1. **Clouds** – Fluffy, billowy shapes made of water droplets that drift across the sky.  
2. **The Sun** – Our nearest star, shining brightly during the day and providing warmth and light.  
3. **The Moon** – Often visible at night (and sometimes during the day), with its changing phases.  
4. **Stars** – Tiny points of light that appear after sunset, especially on clear nights.  
5. **Birds** – Various species of birds soaring or flying overhead, from tiny sparrows to large eagles.  

These are just a few of the many fascinating sights you can observe when you look up at the sky!
