Here are four common types of clouds:

1. **Cirrus** – Thin, wispy clouds found high in the atmosphere, often made of ice crystals.  
2. **Cumulus** – Puffy, white clouds with flat bases and rounded tops, usually seen on fair‑weather days.  
3. **Stratus** – Low, gray, sheet‑like clouds that can cover the sky and sometimes bring drizzle.  
4. **Nimbus (or Nimbostratus)** – Thick, dark clouds associated with steady rain or snow.
