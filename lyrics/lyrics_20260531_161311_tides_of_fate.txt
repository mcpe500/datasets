**Verse 1**  
Moon‑lit waters whisper low,  
A promise written in the sand,  
The tide pulls back, then forward flows,  
A destiny traced by the hand.  

**Verse 2**  
Each wave a step, each foam a sigh,  
The sea remembers every choice,  
When currents turn, we learn to fly,  
And hear the pulse of fate’s own voice.  

**Verse 3**  
Salt‑kissed sunrise paints the sky,  
The horizon bends beneath our feet,  
We walk the line where worlds collide,  
And taste the bittersweet defeat.  

**Verse 4**  
Through storm and calm, the tide will stay,  
It carries us to unknown shores,  
In every pull, a silent prayer,  
That destiny finds us evermore.  

**Chorus**  
Tides of fate, they rise and fall,  
Carrying hope on silver streams,  
We ride the swell, we heed the call,  
And drift toward the endless dream.
