The tallest mountain measured from sea‑level is **Mount Everest**.  

- **Elevation:** 8,848.86 m (29,031.7 ft) – the official height was revised in 2020 by a joint Nepal‑China survey.  
- **Location:** Straddles the border between Nepal (Sagarmatha Zone) and the Tibet Autonomous Region of China.  
- **First ascent:** Sir Edmund Hillary (New Zealand) and Tenzing Norgay (Nepal) reached the summit on May 29 1953.  

If you consider “tallest” as the distance from a mountain’s base to its summit (including the portion underwater), the volcano **Mauna Kea** in Hawaii is actually higher (about 10,203 m from base to summit). However, in everyday usage “the tallest mountain in the world” refers to Everest’s height above sea level.
