| Language | Approximate total speakers (native + second‑language) | Approximate native‑speaker count |

*Sources (2023‑2024 estimates): Ethnologue, UNESCO, and national census data.*

- **English** leads when you add together people who speak it as a native language and those who use it as a second or foreign language. It is the dominant language of international business, diplomacy, science, and the internet.
- **Mandarin Chinese** has the largest number of native speakers, but its reach outside China is much smaller than English’s.

So, depending on how you count, the answer can differ:

- **Most native speakers:** Mandarin Chinese.  
- **Most total speakers (native + second‑language):** English.
