The **cheetah** (Acinonyx jubatus) holds the title of the fastest land animal. It can sprint at speeds of roughly **80–100 km/h (50–62 mph)** over short distances—usually a few hundred meters.  

*Key facts about cheetah speed*  

| Metric | Typical value |
|--------|---------------|
| Top speed | ~80–100 km/h (50–62 mph) |
| Acceleration | 0 to 60 mph in about 3 seconds |
| Distance of top speed | Usually 200–500 m, rarely more than 1 km |
| Time at top speed | A few seconds to a minute |

For comparison, the **pronghorn** (Antilocapra americana) is the second‑fastest land animal, capable of sustained speeds around 55 mph (≈ 88 km/h) over longer distances, but it cannot match the cheetah’s short‑burst sprint speed.
