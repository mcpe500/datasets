**Verse 1**  
Morning light slips through the kitchen blinds,  
The scent of coffee wraps the room in time.  
A worn‑out chair remembers every sigh,  
And the echo of my mother’s lullaby.

**Verse 2**  
Down the lane the old oak stands guard,  
Its leaves a whisper, a steady retort.  
I chased the horizon, tasted distant seas,  
But my heart kept returning to the porch breeze.

**Verse 3**  
The fire crackles, stories intertwined,  
Your laugh in the attic, a warm reminder.  
When the world outside turns cold and unclear,  
The glow of the hearth draws loved ones near.

**Verse 4**  
We gather round the table, candles alight,  
Our laughter rings through the quiet night.  
Strangers become kin in these humble walls,  
Home is the beat that never falls.

**Chorus**  
Home isn’t just walls or a roof above,  
It’s the pulse of love, the bond we share.  
When doubts rise and the road feels long,  
I find my heart anchored right here, belonging.
