The Pacific Ocean is the largest ocean on Earth. It covers roughly **63 million square miles** (about **165 million square kilometers**) and stretches from the western coast of the Americas to the eastern coasts of Asia and Australia. 

Key facts about the Pacific Ocean:

- **Area:** ~165 million km² (≈ 63 million mi²), which is larger than all the landmasses on Earth combined.  
- **Volume:** Approximately 710 million cubic kilometers of water.  
- **Depth:** The deepest point on Earth, the Challenger Deep in the Mariana Trench, is about **10,935 meters (35,853 feet)** deep.  
- **Bordering continents:** North America, South America, Asia, Australia, and many islands in the Pacific.  

It’s roughly twice the size of the next‑largest ocean, the Atlantic Ocean, and represents about 46% of the world’s ocean surface.
