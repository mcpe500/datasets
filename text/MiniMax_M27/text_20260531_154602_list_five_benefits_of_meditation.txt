**Five benefits of meditation**

1. **Stress reduction** – Regular meditation helps lower cortisol levels and activates the relaxation response, making everyday pressures feel more manageable.  
2. **Improved focus and attention** – Practices such as mindfulness train the brain to sustain attention, which can enhance concentration and reduce mind‑wandering.  
3. **Emotional well‑being** – Meditation encourages greater awareness of thoughts and feelings, fostering healthier responses, reduced anxiety, and a more positive mood.  
4. **Better sleep** – By calming the nervous system and quieting racing thoughts, meditation can improve sleep quality and help you fall asleep more quickly.  
5. **Enhanced self‑awareness** – Techniques like loving‑kindness or body‑scan meditation promote a deeper connection with yourself, leading to clearer personal insight and growth.
