**Verse 1**  
Open roads stretch before me,  
Maps are drawn in the dust of desire.  
Every step a whispered promise,  
A chance to turn the tide of fate.

**Verse 2**  
Morning light rides the horizon,  
Casting shadows that fade in my wake.  
I hear the wind call my name,  
Inviting the heart to pick its own path.

**Verse 3**  
Choices flutter like leaves in the storm,  
Each one a brushstroke on the canvas of time.  
When I hold the reins of my own will,  
The world opens wide, alive and unbound.

**Verse 4**  
Footprints on the sand fade,  
Yet the echo of decision remains.  
Freedom is not a silent promise,  
But a chorus of actions I choose to sing.

**Chorus**  
I am the key, the lock, the open door,  
Every crossroads a chance to roar.  
In the garden of choices I roam,  
Freedom blossoms—my heart’s true home.
