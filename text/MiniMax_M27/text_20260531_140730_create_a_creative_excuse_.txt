Here’s one you can riff on:

**“I was stuck in a time‑loop of my own making – I kept hitting snooze, but each time I woke up, the clock reset to 5 minutes earlier. By the time I finally broke the cycle, the universe decided to send me a free coffee voucher… but the line at the café was longer than the line for the Tardis.”**  

Feel free to tweak it to fit the situation (maybe swap the coffee for a “free donut” or the “Tardis” for any fictional portal you like). A little whimsy can turn an ordinary “I’m late” into a memorable story!
