**Stories Untold**  

*Verse 1*  
In the hush of midnight, whispers linger,  
Pages rustle, unseen and unsaid,  
Shadows curl around forgotten memories,  
A chorus of voices that never was read.  

*Chorus*  
These are the stories untold,  
Unfolding in the silence of the night,  
They breathe in the cracks of our hearts,  
Waiting for a spark, a listening light.  

*Verse 2*  
Dusty tomes line the attic’s quiet shelves,  
Their spines cracked, titles faded away,  
Tales that once danced in firelight, now lost,  
Echoing a truth the world can’t display.  

*Chorus*  
These are the stories untold,  
Unfolding in the silence of the night,  
They breathe in the cracks of our hearts,  
Waiting for a spark, a listening light.  

*Verse 3*  
A child’s laughter once floated on the wind,  
A lover’s sigh sang through rustling leaves,  
They linger in the spaces between breaths,  
Calling us to hear what the night conceives.  

*Chorus*  
These are the stories untold,  
Unfolding in the silence of the night,  
They breathe in the cracks of our hearts,  
Waiting for a spark, a listening light.  

*Verse 4*  
When we pause, close our eyes, open our ears,  
The unheard narratives rise, luminous, bright,  
They weave new worlds from the threads of the past,  
Turning silence into a shining sunrise.  

*Chorus* (final)  
These are the stories untold,  
Unfolding in the silence of the night,  
They breathe in the cracks of our hearts,  
Waiting for a spark, a listening light.
