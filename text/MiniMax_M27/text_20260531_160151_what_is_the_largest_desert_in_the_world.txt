The **largest desert on Earth** isn’t a scorching sea of sand—it’s **Antarctica**.  

- **Antarctica** is a **cold desert**. It receives very little precipitation (mostly snow) and covers roughly **14.2 million km² (5.5 million mi²)**, making it the biggest desert by area.

If you’re thinking of **hot deserts**, the **Sahara** in North Africa holds that title, spanning about **9.2 million km² (3.6 million mi²)**.  

So, depending on how “desert” is defined (by climate type or temperature), the answer can be:

| Desert | Type | Approximate Area |
|--------|------|-----------------|
| Antarctica | Cold desert (polar) | ~14.2 million km² |
| Sahara | Hot desert (subtropical) | ~9.2 million km² |

Both are considered the largest of their respective categories.
