**Verse 1**  
The tide of my feelings swells and sings,  
A surge that lifts me up to sky,  
Then pulls me down on silver wings,  
A rhythm that won’t let me hide.  

**Chorus**  
Waves of emotion, rise and fall,  
Carrying my heart through the storm,  
In every swell I hear the call,  
To surf the tide, to stay warm.  

**Verse 2**  
From gentle ripples to a roaring sea,  
Each wave a memory, a breath,  
It crashes on the walls inside of me,  
Leaving salt upon my skin.  

**Verse 3**  
When the storm runs high I close my eyes,  
And let the water wrap my soul,  
The foam of laughter, tears that rise,  
A quiet storm that makes me whole.  

**Verse 4**  
Now the waves retreat, the sun appears,  
The sea settles into a glass‑bright calm,  
I stand upon the shore of years,  
And smile as the horizon calms.
