**Verse 1**  
Beneath a waning moon, the hourglass turns,  
Each grain a heartbeat, a memory that yearns,  
They drift like shadows, soft as a sigh,  
And paint the dunes with amber light.  

**Chorus**  
We are the sands of time, ever flowing,  
Each grain a story, endlessly growing,  
We rise and fall with the rhythm of years,  
Leaving footprints of hope amid tears.  

**Verse 2**  
When sunrise kisses the dunes, they awaken,  
Each grain a moment, gently unshaken,  
They whisper of lovers who passed like wind,  
Now linger in horizons we have yet to begin.  

**Chorus**  
We are the sands of time, ever flowing,  
Each grain a story, endlessly growing,  
We rise and fall with the rhythm of years,
