**Verse 1**  
When the night wraps tight around my bones,  
I hear a whisper through the cold—  
a distant drum that steadies my stride,  
a promise that the dark can’t hide.  

**Verse 2**  
Every stumble is a stepping stone,  
each cracked road a story‑line sewn.  
I lift my eyes beyond the broken sky,  
where stubborn seeds of hope still lie.  

**Verse 3**  
The wind may push, the rain may pour,  
but I’m a river carving stone,  
I bend, I twist, I never quit,  
for perseverance fuels my grit.  

**Verse 4**  
When dawn finally breaks its amber veil,  
the sunrise crowns my weary trail.  
I stand on hills of every fallen try,  
and smile—because I learned to fly.  

**Chorus**  
Rise up, rise up, let the fire in you burn,  
through every storm, keep the flame you’ve earned.  
Hope is the echo that never dies,  
the song inside that lifts us to the skies.
