**Verse 1**  
Morning mist rolls off the dock, the tide a steady hum,  
A weathered hull awaits the wake, the compass points to “somewhere.”  
The crew shouts “All hands!” as sails unfurl in the rising sun,  
And the ship departs the harbor, chasing horizons in the air.  

**Chorus**  
Oh, let the ocean sing, the salt‑kissed wind be free,  
Through billows bright and dark we ride, the world beneath the sea.  
We chart a path on rolling waves, where legends dare to be—  
In every sunrise, every storm, the sea calls you and me.  

**Verse 2**  
The night sky folds in indigo, the stars are compass‑light,  
While phosphorescent swells ignite the deep like scattered fire.  
We weather gales with heartbeats drum, the rigging sings in spite,  
And every swell a promise made, a whispered, steadfast choir.  

**Verse 3**  
Islands rise like ancient myths, their shores of gold and green,  
We drop the anchor, taste the breeze, hear parrots’ morning call.  
The sea‑foam whispers secrets old, the sand a hallowed screen,  
And in the quietude we find that wanderers never fall.  

**Verse 4**  
When daylight fades and lanterns glow, the ship turns back to port,  
The harbor lights a familiar glow, the crew’s heartbeats steady slow.  
The ocean’s tale is written on our hands, a permanent short,  
A yearning for the next horizon, where the endless currents flow.
