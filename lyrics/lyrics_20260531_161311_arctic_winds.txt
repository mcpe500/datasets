**Verse 1**  
The wind rolls down from the far‑northern sky,  
A whisper of ice as it passes me by,  
It carries the scent of a frozen sunrise,  
And stirs up the silence where the snowflakes lie.  

**Verse 2**  
Through valleys of white where the reindeers roam,  
It sings ancient songs, a melodic cold home,  
It paints the horizon with frost‑laden glow,  
And whispers of stories that centuries know.  

**Verse 3**  
My breath turns to mist in its sharp, bitter kiss,  
The world becomes glass in the wind’s icy bliss,  
It lifts the old memories, scatters them wide,  
Like stars falling down where the cold waters hide.  

**Verse 4**  
Yet even the harshness can’t dim the bright spark,  
The Arctic wind carries a fierce, tender bark,  
It calls to the dreamers, the brave, and the lost,  
To chase after horizons at whatever cost.  

**Chorus**  
Arctic winds, blow through my soul,  
Bring the chill, but let me feel whole,  
With every gust, the world rewrites,  
In frozen light, we find our lights.
