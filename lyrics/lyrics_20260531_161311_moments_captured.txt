**Verse 1**  
Sunset paints the sky in amber light,  
We pause the world, a second feels right.  
A click of camera, the moment stays,  
A snapshot frozen in golden haze.  

**Verse 2**  
Your laughter rings like chimes in summer breeze,  
Your eyes alight, a story in the trees.  
We frame the joy, a memory to keep,  
Like a ribbon tied in endless sleep.  

**Verse 3**  
Raindrops dance on the window pane,  
The world outside, a soft refrain.  
In that quiet beat, we find our grace,  
A fleeting scene we learn to embrace.  

**Verse 4**  
Midnight whispers, stars start to glow,  
Time may wander, but these moments flow.  
We store them deep in heart’s own vault,  
A timeless echo that never halts.  

**Chorus**  
Moments captured, they linger on,  
In the quiet of the night they’re drawn.  
They glow like embers, bright and warm,  
A melody that never loses its form.
