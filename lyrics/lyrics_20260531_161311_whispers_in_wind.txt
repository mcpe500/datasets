**Verse 1**  
Morning dew hangs on the trembling leaves,  
A gentle breeze begins to hum.  
It carries whispers that the night bequeaths,  
Like verses soft that slowly come.  

**Verse 2**  
In twilight’s amber glow the wind draws near,  
A secret song from ages past.  
It curls around the willows, low and clear,  
And stirs the heart of those who cast.  

**Verse 3**  
Through corridors of rustling ancient trees,  
The breeze weaves threads of silver sound.  
It tells of distant hopes on distant seas,  
And spins a love both firm and bound.  

**Verse 4**  
When night deepens and the stars take light,  
The whispers rise, a lullaby.  
They echo soft beneath the fading night,  
And paint the sky with sighs that sigh.  

**Chorus**  
Oh, whispers in the wind, you guide my soul,  
A fleeting song that never dies.  
Your breath of magic makes the lost feel whole,  
And lift our spirits to the endless skies.
