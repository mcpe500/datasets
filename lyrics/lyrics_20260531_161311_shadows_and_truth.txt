**Verse 1**  
In midnight’s hush the silhouettes align,  
They stretch and curl like thoughts we keep inside,  
Each curve a secret, each angle a lie,  
The shadows dance but never speak our name.  

**Verse 2**  
When daylight breaks, the truth steps through the door,  
It splinters the dark that held us in its grip,  
And where the shadow fell, a story’s left to explore,  
Our hidden selves now glow beneath the sun’s script.  

**Verse 3**  
The edge where night meets dawn is thin as breath,  
There shadows linger, trembling with untold,  
They whisper half‑truths, a promise or a death,  
And truth steps gently, pulling back the fold.  

**Verse 4**  
So we walk with both, the dusk and the bright,  
The shadows guard the depths where our truths reside,  
In their silent watch, we find the quiet might,  
To love the light, to hear the dark, to trust the tide.  

**Chorus**  
Shadows whisper truth, the silent kind,  
They echo what the heart already knows,  
In darkness we discover what we find,  
The truth that glows where shadow’s silence grows.
