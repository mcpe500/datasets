**Verse 1**  
Under a blazing sun that never sleeps,  
The desert spreads its golden dunes, endless sweeps.  
Each grain of sand tells a story of time,  
A silent hymn of wind that shapes every climb.  

**Chorus**  
Oh, desert sands, you shimmer and sway,  
A sea of amber that holds the day.  
In your quiet roar I find my way,  
Dancing with the heat, we sway and stay.

**Verse 4**  
Morning returns with a blush of rose,  
The first light paints the crests, a gentle pose.
