**Bridges Built**

*Verse 1*  
From river’s edge to sunrise glow,  
they laid the stones where water flows.  
Each keystone placed, a promise made,  
to span the distance, unafraid.  

*Chorus*  
We build bridges, hand in hand,  
linking shores across the land.  
With every beam and every span,  
we turn the tide, we make a plan.  

*Verse 2*  
Across the canyon, steel and light,  
the city’s heart beats through the night.  
Voices echo on the wire,  
as strangers meet and empires rise higher.  

*Chorus*  
We build bridges, hand in hand,  
linking shores across the land.  
With every beam and every span,  
we turn the tide, we make a plan.  

*Verse 3*  
Where storms once roared and currents swirled,  
the arching path becomes a world.  
No wall can hold the need to stay,  
the bridge remembers every ray.  

*Chorus*  
We build bridges, hand in hand,  
linking shores across the land.  
With every beam and every span,  
we turn the tide, we make a plan.  

*Verse 4*  
From ancient stone to neon glow,  
the bridge endures, the seeds we sow.  
Future footsteps cross the seam,  
a lasting song of gathered dreams.  

*Chorus*  
We build bridges, hand in hand,  
linking shores across the land.
