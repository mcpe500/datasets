Here are three widely celebrated painters:

1. **Leonardo da Vinci** – Italian Renaissance master known for works such as the *Mona Lisa* and *The Last Supper*.  
2. **Pablo Picasso** – Spanish artist who co‑founded the Cubist movement and created iconic pieces like *Guernica* and *Les Demoiselles d'Avignon*.  
3. **Vincent van Gogh** – Dutch post‑impressionist painter whose vivid, emotive works include *Starry Night* and *The Sunflowers*.
