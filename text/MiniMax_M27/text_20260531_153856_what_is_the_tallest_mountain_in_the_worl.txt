The tallest mountain in the world—measured by elevation above sea level—is **Mount Everest**. It rises to 8,848 meters (29,029 feet) and lies in the Himalayas on the border between Nepal and the Tibet Autonomous Region of China (often referred to as “Sagarmatha” in Nepali and “Chomolungma” in Tibetan).  

*(If you consider the total height from a mountain’s base to its summit, including the part submerged underwater, Mauna Kea in Hawaii would be the tallest, but in everyday usage “tallest” refers to elevation above sea level, for which Everest holds the record.)*
