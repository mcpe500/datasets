**Verse 1**  
The night was heavy, but a new dawn glows,  
Through shattered doubts a brighter horizon grows,  
Each breath I take fuels the fire inside,  
I rise again with hope as my guide.  

**Verse 2**  
Old shadows fall as I step into day,  
The bruises fade, my spirit starts to sway,  
With every step the weight lifts from my core,  
Strength reborn, I crave for more.  

**Verse 3**  
I stitch the pieces of the broken road,  
A melody of courage starts to unfold,  
No longer tethered by the fear of pain,  
I find the power that lets me reign.  

**Verse 4**  
From ashes rise the phoenix of my soul,  
Each challenge faced becomes a part of the whole,  
Renewed and steady, I chase the infinite sky,  
Unbreakable, I lift my voice on high.  

**Chorus**  
Strength renewed, I stand unchained,  
Burning bright, my heart untamed.  
Rise above the doubts and the night,  
Together we rise, we shine, we light.
