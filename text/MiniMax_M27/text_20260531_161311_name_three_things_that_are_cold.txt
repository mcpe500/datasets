Here are three things that are commonly described as cold:

1. **Ice cubes** – frozen water that’s well below the freezing point.  
2. **A snow‑covered mountain peak** – high‑altitude areas where temperatures can drop far below 0 °C (32 °F).  
3. **A chilled beverage** – a drink that has been placed in a refrigerator or over ice to lower its temperature.
