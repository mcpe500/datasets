**Verse 1**  
When the sunrise paints the sky in gold,  
Shadows shrink, the world feels bold.  
But as dusk falls, the darkness creeps,  
A quiet hush where the silence keeps.  

**Verse 2**  
In the midnight, the stars take the stage,  
Each twinkle a whisper, a distant gauge.  
Light flickers soft in a candle’s glow,  
A beacon that guides where the night winds blow.  

**Verse 3**  
We dance between the bright and the black,  
Finding balance on a tight‑rope track.  
Joy can bloom where shadows reside,  
And hope can sprout from the darkest tide.  

**Verse 4**  
When the world feels heavy, the night feels long,  
Remember the dawn will right every wrong.  
The darkness is a canvas, the light a brush,  
Together they paint the heart’s rush.  

**Chorus**  
Light and darkness, a duet in the night,  
One gives us hope, the other, the fight.  
In the shadows we learn to see,  
And in the glow we find we’re free.
