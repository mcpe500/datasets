**Verse 1**  
Morning coffee steam, the kitchen still,  
A pause before the word, a quiet thrill.  
The clink of a spoon, then silence sings—  
A story in the hush that brightens everything.  

**Verse 2**  
Two strangers on a platform, eyes lock in,  
No words pass through, yet hearts begin to spin.  
The train’s distant roar fades to a sigh,  
The silence there tells a story deep and high.  

**Verse 3**  
When the storm rolls out, the world turns mute,  
Raindrops tap the window in a soft salute.  
The quiet after thunder holds a whispered prayer—  
The silence that speaks, a calm that fills the air.  

**Verse 4**  
Under the moonlight, we sit hand in hand,  
Words fall short, yet love expands.  
The crickets’ chirp is just a subtle beat—  
In this stillness, our souls meet.  

**Chorus**  
In the spaces where no voices rise,  
A quiet chorus of unspoken sighs.  
Silences that speak, they carry the key,  
To every story that lives in you and me.
