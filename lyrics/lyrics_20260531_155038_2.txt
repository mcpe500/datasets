**Verse 1 – Spring**  
Budding leaves catch the morning light,  
Birds return, their songs take flight.  
Rain kisses earth, the blossoms wake,  
A fresh‑green quilt the hillsides make.  

**Verse 2 – Summer**  
Sun‑kissed fields turn golden bright,  
Children laugh where rivers glide.  
Fireflies spark the dusk’s soft veil,  
Warm evenings tell a timeless tale.  

**Verse 3 – Autumn**  
Crisp wind paints the maples red,  
Harvest moon above the shed.  
Leaves descend like whispered dreams,  
Nature sighs as daylight gleams.  

**Verse 4 – Winter**  
Snow drifts drape the silent woods,  
Frosty breath in quiet moods.  
Bare branches hold the crystal sky,  
A peaceful hush as seasons die.  

**Chorus**  
Through the turning wheel we sway,  
Spring to summer, fall to gray,  
Nature’s chorus sings our name—  
Endless cycle, ever‑same.
