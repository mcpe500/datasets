**Verse 1**  
Morning light is spilling gold,  
Laughter rolls like waves of old,  
Every heart is beating fast,  
We’re all invited to the cast.  

**Verse 2**  
Friends are gathering in the square,  
Music lifts us through the air,  
Dancing feet on cobblestones,  
Echoes of the joy we’ve known.  

**Verse 3**  
Colors burst in every hue,  
Smiles appear in morning dew,  
Raise a glass, let spirits rise,  
Celebrate beneath the skies.  

**Verse 4**  
Time stands still, the world is bright,  
Every soul ignite the light,  
Sing along, the chorus swells,  
Joy is ringing like the bells.  

**Chorus**  
We’re dancing, we’re free, we’re alive,  
With every beat our spirits thrive.  
Let the joy flow, let the music play,  
Celebrate today—forever and a day!
