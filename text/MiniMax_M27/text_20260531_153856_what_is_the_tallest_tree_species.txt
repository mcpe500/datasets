The **coast redwood** (*Sequoia sempervirens*) is the tallest tree species in the world. The tallest known living specimen, named **Hyperion**, stands at approximately **380 feet (115.9 meters)** and is located in Redwood National Park, California, USA.

These trees are renowned for their immense height, slender silhouette, and ability to thrive in the foggy, temperate rainforests of the Pacific coast. Other tall species include the **mountain ash** (*Eucalyptus regnans*) in Australia and the **giant sequoia** (*Sequoiadendron giganteum*), though the latter is the most massive in volume but not the tallest.
