The hottest planet in our Solar System is **Venus**.  

- **Average surface temperature:** ≈ 462 °C (≈ 864 °F)  
- **Why it’s hotter than Mercury (the closest planet to the Sun):** Venus has a very thick atmosphere composed mainly of carbon dioxide with clouds of sulfuric acid. This creates a runaway greenhouse effect that traps solar heat, raising surface temperatures well above those of Mercury, which lacks a significant atmosphere.  

So despite being farther from the Sun than Mercury, Venus holds the title of the hottest planet.
