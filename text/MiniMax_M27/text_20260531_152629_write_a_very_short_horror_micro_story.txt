The last occupant of the old mill had vanished without a trace, leaving behind only a dusty diary and a single word scrawled on the wall: “still”. 

When I moved in, I thought the silence was peaceful—until the night the kitchen faucet turned on by itself, and a cold breath brushed my ear as the words “still here” echoed from every empty room. I fled, slamming the door behind me, but the lock clicked shut before I could escape. 

A whisper rose from the walls, “You’re just the next page.”
