Here are four common cloud types:

1. **Cumulus** – puffy, white clouds with flat bases, often seen on fair‑weather days.  
2. **Stratus** – low, gray, sheet‑like clouds that can cover the sky and sometimes produce drizzle.  
3. **Cirrus** – thin, wispy clouds made of ice crystals, found high in the atmosphere.  
4. **Cumulonimbus** – tall, dense clouds associated with thunderstorms, heavy rain, and sometimes hail.
