**Verse 1**  
Lost in the silence, a road that won’t end,  
Heart buried deep where the shadows descend.  
A whisper grew soft, like a hand reaching in,  
Pulled me from darkness, a light breaking in.  

**Verse 2**  
When the clouds cracked open and sunrise poured through,  
A warmth wrapped around me, a grace ever true.  
It taught me to breathe, to forgive, to embrace,  
A gentle compassion that never leaves a trace.  

**Verse 3**  
I reached for the promise, my spirit set free,  
Now standing on mountaintops, bold and care‑free.  
The world sings louder, each note pure and bright,  
Grace is the anthem that guides me to light.  

**Verse 4**  
Every scar tells a story, each tear becomes rain,  
For in the redemption I find my refrain.  
The journey is over, the compass points home,  
Grace found my soul, and I never roam.  

**Chorus**  
Grace found, grace found,  
It lifts the lost and sets the bound,  
In every storm a guiding sound,  
Grace found—my heart is found.
