Here are four of the celebrated **Seven Wonders of the Ancient World**:

1. **Great Pyramid of Giza** – The oldest and largest of the pyramids, built for the Egyptian pharaoh Khufu (c. 2580 BC).  
2. **Hanging Gardens of Babylon** – A tiered, irrigated garden said to have been constructed in ancient Babylon for a queen (c. 600 BC).  
3. **Statue of Zeus at Olympia** – A giant gold‑ and ivory‑covered statue of the god Zeus, created by the sculptor Phidias (c. 435 BC).  
4. **Colossus of Rhodes** – A monumental bronze statue of the sun‑god Helios erected at the harbor of Rhodes (c. 280 BC).
