The speed of light in a vacuum is a fundamental constant:

- **≈ 299,792,458 meters per second (m/s)**
- ≈ 300,000 kilometers per second (km/s)
- ≈ 186,282 miles per second (mi/s)

This value, usually denoted by **c**, is the maximum speed at which all massless particles (including light) can travel through empty space.
