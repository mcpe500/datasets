The tallest mountain **above sea level** is **Mount Everest**.  

- **Elevation:** 8,848.86 m (29,031.7 ft) — the official height as of the 2020 Nepal–China joint survey (including the snow cap).  
- **Location:** Himalayan range on the border of Nepal (Sagarmatha) and the Tibet Autonomous Region of China (Qomolangma).  
- **Coordinates:** 27°59′17″ N, 86°55′31″ E.  

Everest’s summit is at the roof of the world, and it has been the goal of countless climbers since the first confirmed ascent by Sir Edmund Hillary and Tenzing Norgay in 1953.  

*(If you measure a mountain from its base on the ocean floor to its summit, Mauna Kea (in Hawaii) is taller—about 10,203 m (33,474 ft) from base to top—but it rises only about 4,207 m above sea level.)*
