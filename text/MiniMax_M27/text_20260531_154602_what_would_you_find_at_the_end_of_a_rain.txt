A rainbow isn’t a physical object you can walk up to – it’s an optical illusion created by sunlight being refracted, reflected, and dispersed inside countless raindrops.  

**What the “end” really is**

| Aspect | Explanation |
|--------|--------------|
| **Geometry** | From any given observer’s position the rainbow appears as a circular arc (or a full circle if you’re high enough, e.g., in an airplane). The “center” of that circle is always the spot where the observer’s head is, so the “end” you see is simply the point where the arc meets the horizon or a building. |
| **Location‑dependence** | As you move, the rainbow moves with you; the apparent end that seemed just over the next hill is always a little farther away. In other words, there’s no fixed place where a rainbow terminates. |
| **What’s actually there** | Only more raindrops, air, and the same light‑splitting process that creates the colors in the first place. If you could magically appear at the exact spot where the arc seems to end, you’d just see the same colored light refracting through the same droplets you already see. |

**Folklore vs. science**

- **Folklore**: The idea of a pot of gold hidden at the rainbow’s end, guarded by a leprechaun, is a charming myth rooted in Irish legend. It plays on the notion that the rainbow is a reward waiting to be claimed.
- **Science**: The rainbow is a spectrum of colors produced by the dispersion of white light, each color leaving the droplet at a slightly different angle (≈ 42° for the primary rainbow). No treasure is hidden there—only the physics of light and water.

**What you *could* find if you tried to “reach” the rainbow**

1. **More rain** – The same cloud that produced the rainbow is still dripping.
2. **A shifting perspective** – The arc you see will seem to recede as you walk toward it, because you’re moving the centre of the circle.
3. **A pretty good view of the sky** – If the rain stops, the colors fade as the droplets evaporate; you’ll be left with ordinary clouds and sunshine.
4. **A story to tell** – The hunt for the “end” is a timeless adventure, even if the prize is just the memory of a beautiful phenomenon.

So, at the literal “end” of a rainbow you’ll find nothing but water droplets and light—plus, perhaps, a smile from a child who believed they’d discovered a hidden pot of gold. 🌈✨
