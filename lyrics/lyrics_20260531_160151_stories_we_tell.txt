**Verse 1**  
When the night is soft and the firelight glows,  
we spin a tale of kings and dancing crows.  
A whisper of wonder, a promise of gold,  
the world’s first heartbeat, the story we’re told.  

**Verse 2**  
Ancient voices echo through the canyon’s stone,  
heroes and monsters, love‑songs we’ve known.  
From whispered myths to verses carved in sand,  
each legend lives on when we lend our hand.  

**Verse 3**  
We share our days in coffee‑shop chatter,  
the broken‑heart memoir, the hopeful‑laugh‑after.  
Every smile, each tear, a thread in the loom,  
the stories we tell paint the rooms of our boom.
