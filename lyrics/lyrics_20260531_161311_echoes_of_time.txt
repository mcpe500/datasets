**Verse 1**  
In the hush of twilight, the old clock chimes,  
A whisper of moments that have slipped through time.  
Footsteps fade on cobblestones, the past still lingers,  
Echoes of laughter, of sorrow, of lingering fingers.  

**Verse 2**  
Memories ripple like stones dropped in a pond,  
Their ripples reach the shore where our futures are drawn.  
Each heartbeat a metronome, ticking in the night,  
Repeating the stories that refuse to take flight.  

**Verse 3**  
Silver screens flicker with shadows of yesterday,  
A chorus of voices humming a soft lullaby.  
The wind carries the refrains of forgotten dreams,  
Carrying them forward, adorning our seams.  

**Verse 4**  
When dawn breaks, the echoes dissolve into light,  
Leaving traces of love that never truly die.  
We walk onward, carrying the past in our stride,  
A timeless echo, forever by our side.  

**Chorus**  
Echoes of time, ringing in the void,  
We hear the past, we feel the future unfold.  
In every breath, a rhythm of old and new,  
Echoes of time, forever guiding you.
