Here are three widely recognized inventors:

1. **Thomas Edison** – Known for developing the practical incandescent light bulb and the phonograph, as well as establishing the first electrical power distribution system.  
2. **Nikola Tesla** – Pioneered alternating‑current (AC) electrical systems, radio technology, and many aspects of modern electric power generation and transmission.  
3. **Alexander Graham Bell** – Credited with inventing the first practical telephone, which laid the groundwork for global telecommunications.
