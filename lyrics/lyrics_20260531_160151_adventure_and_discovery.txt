**Verse 1**  
The sunrise paints the horizon gold,  
A distant drum of thunder calls the bold.  
We lace our boots, step past the gate,  
Leaving behind the safe, the known, the late.  

**Verse 2**  
Through tangled woods and whispered streams,  
We chase the echo of forgotten dreams.  
Each turn reveals a secret sign,  
A map drawn in the heart, not in the mind.  

**Verse 3**  
The mountain’s crown is veiled in mist,  
A promise that the view, once missed, islist.  
We climb with fire inside our chest,  
Where every stone becomes a test.  

**Verse 4**  
At dusk we find a valley bright,  
Where stars ignite the endless night.  
Our footprints mark the path we’ve blazed,  
A story written, never phased.  

**Chorus**  
Adventure sings, discovery rings,  
We rise and roam on boundless wings.  
The world awakes where curiosity leads,  
In every step, a new world breeds.
