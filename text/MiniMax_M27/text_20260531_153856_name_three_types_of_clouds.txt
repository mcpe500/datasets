Sure! Here are three common types of clouds:

1. **Cumulus** – puffy, white clouds with flat bases, often seen on fair‑weather days.  
2. **Stratus** – low, gray, layered clouds that can bring overcast skies and light drizzle.  
3. **Cirrus** – high, wispy, hair‑like clouds made of ice crystals, usually appearing in fair weather.
