**“Frisky Fisher Fred’s Fantastic Fin‑Flip”**

Frisky Fisher Fred found five fresh fish,
Fiercely flapping, fizzing, flipping on the dish.
Furious Fred frantically tried to flip the fish,
Fearing the fierce fin‑flick would fizz and freeze his wish.

Fingers fumbled, foamy foam flew fast,
Frogs and finches fled the frantic, flashing cast.
Finally, Fred flipped the fish with a flip‑flop fling,
Frothing, frosted, and finally free for a sing.
