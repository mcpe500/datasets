The **cheetah** (*Acinonyx jubatus*) is generally recognized as the fastest land animal, capable of sprinting at speeds of about **112 km h⁻¹ (≈70 mph)** over short distances. It can accelerate from 0 to 96 km h⁻¹ in just a few seconds, thanks to its lightweight body, enlarged nasal passages, and specialized limb musculature that provides exceptional stride length and rapid leg turnover.

*Other notably fast land animals include:*  
- **Pronghorn** (up to ~88 km h⁻¹, 55 mph) – the second‑fastest land animal, built for sustained high‑speed running.  
- **Springbok** and **blue wildebeest** (around 80–90 km h⁻¹) – also among the quickest, though still behind the cheetah.

So, if you’re looking for the record‑holder for sheer top speed on land, the cheetah takes the crown.
