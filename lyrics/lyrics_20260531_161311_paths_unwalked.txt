**Verse 1**  
The morning mist hangs low, the road splits in two,  
I stand on the edge where the wild winds blew.  
Each step I didn’t take is a whisper in the night,  
A silent promise of the life that never took flight.  

**Verse 2**  
I trace the map of choices I left behind,  
Their faded ink a echo of what I could find.  
In the quiet woods the leaves still hold the sound,  
Of footsteps that never fell on that untouched ground.  

**Verse 3**  
The city lights flicker, a glow I never chased,  
The alleys unexplored where my shadow was displaced.  
Every “what‑if” sings a song I can’t ignore,  
A chorus of roads that fade into myth and lore.  

**Verse 4**  
Now the sun begins to set on the paths unwalked,  
Yet I feel their pull, like a thread that’s never断了.  
I carry the ache of the miles I didn’t roam,  
And the promise that someday I’ll make them my home.  

**Chorus**  
Paths unwalked, they call to me,  
Silent roads where I could be.  
In the echo of the steps I missed,  
I find the strength to dream what’s left.
