The speed of light in a vacuum, denoted **c**, is exactly  

\[
c = 299{,}792{,}458\ \text{meters per second}
\]

(about \(3.00 \times 10^{8}\) m s⁻¹, or roughly \(1.08 \times 10^{9}\) km h⁻¹).  

Because the meter is now defined by fixing the speed of light, this value is exact and does not change with experimental measurements.
