The smallest country in the world is **Vatican City** (Status Civitatis Vaticanae).

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Area** | ≈ 0.44 km² (44 hectares) |
| **Population** | About 800 people (as of 2023) |
| **Location** | Enclave within Rome, Italy |
| **Government** | Absolute theocratic monarchy (the Pope) |
| **Official languages** | Latin (official), Italian (working) |
| **Currency** | Euro (€) |
| **Key facts** | • Smallest both by area and by population.<br>• Home to the Roman Catholic Church’s central administration, including St. Peter’s Basilica, the Sistine Chapel, and the Vatican Museums.<br>• A sovereign state under the Treaty of the Lateran (1929). |

If you’re interested in other tiny states, the next‑smallest by area are:

1. **Monaco** – 2.02 km²  
2. **Nauru** – 21.3 km² (but Nauru is the smallest island nation)  
3. **Tuvalu** – 26 km²  
4. **San Marino** – 61 km²  
5. **Liechtenstein** – 160 km²  

All of these are much larger than Vatican City, which remains the world’s smallest independent nation.
