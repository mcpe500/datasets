Here are three examples of things that are typically very loud:

1. **Thunder** – the booming sound from a lightning strike can be heard from miles away.  
2. **A jet engine** – especially when an aircraft takes off, it produces an extremely high‑decibel roar.  
3. **A rock concert** – amplified music, crowds cheering, and the overall ambiance combine to create sound levels well above safe listening limits.
