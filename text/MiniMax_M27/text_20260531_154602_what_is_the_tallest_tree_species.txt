The world’s tallest tree species is the **coast redwood** (*Sequoia sempervirens*). The tallest known living individual, a specimen called **Hyperion**, stands about **115 m (≈ 380 ft)** tall. 

For context, other very tall species include:

| Species | Typical max height* |
|---------|-------------------|
| **Coast redwood** (*Sequoia sempervirens*) | 115 m (≈ 380 ft) |
| **Mountain ash** (*Eucalyptus regnans*) | ~100 m (≈ 330 ft) |
| **Douglas‑fir** (*Pseudotsuga menziesii*) | ~90 m (≈ 295 ft) |
| **Sitka spruce** (*Picea sitchensis*) | ~90 m (≈ 295 ft) |

*“Typical max height” refers to the tallest reliably recorded individuals for each species.

So, if you’re looking for the tallest tree species on Earth, the coast redwood takes the record.
