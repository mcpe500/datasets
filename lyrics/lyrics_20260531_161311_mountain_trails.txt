**Verse 1**  
The pine‑scented wind blows soft through the pass,  
Where sunrise paints the ridge in gold and glass.  
Each step on the trail feels like a whispered prayer,  
A winding path that calls me to go there.

**Chorus**  
Oh, mountain trails, you draw my soul,  
Through granite cliffs and mist you roll.  
With every bend, my heart takes flight,  
In the quiet wild, I find my light.
