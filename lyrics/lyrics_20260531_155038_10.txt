**Verse 1**  
Old brick walls echo my first laugh,  
A kitchen humming with mother’s song.  
Every step on the porch feels like a map,  
Tracing the path where I belong.  

**Chorus**  
Home’s not just a place on a street,  
It’s the pulse that steadies my beat.  
When the world pulls far and wide,  
It’s the heart inside that stays my guide.
