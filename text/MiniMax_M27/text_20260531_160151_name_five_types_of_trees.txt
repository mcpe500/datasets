Here are five common types of trees:

1. **Oak** – a large, deciduous tree known for its strong wood and distinctive lobed leaves.  
2. **Pine** – an evergreen conifer with needle‑like leaves and woody cones; many species are valued for timber and resin.  
3. **Maple** – a deciduous genus famous for its palmate leaves and vibrant fall colors; also the source of maple syrup.  
4. **Birch** – a deciduous tree often recognized by its papery, white bark and serrated leaves; common in cooler climates.  
5. **Eucalyptus** – an evergreen tree (or shrub) native to Australia, known for its aromatic leaves and rapid growth.
