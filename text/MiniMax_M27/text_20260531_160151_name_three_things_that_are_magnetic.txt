Here are three examples of things that are magnetic:

1. **Iron** – a ferromagnetic metal that is strongly attracted to magnets.  
2. **Neodymium magnets** – a type of rare‑earth magnet used in many high‑strength applications (e.g., hard‑drive motors, magnetic closures).  
3. **Compass needle** – a small magnetized piece of steel that aligns with Earth’s magnetic field, allowing the compass to point north.
