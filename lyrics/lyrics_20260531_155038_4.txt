**Verse 1**  
Sunrise paints the sky in gold,  
Children’s laughter, stories told,  
Footsteps drum on streets of light,  
Joy ignites the morning bright.  

**Verse 2**  
Music swells, the crowd will roar,  
Every heart beats at the core,  
Balloons rise, colors fill the air,  
Celebration spreads everywhere.  

**Verse 3**  
Friends gather close, hand in hand,  
Dancing circles on the sand,  
Candles flicker, wishes bright,  
Warmth embraces all the night.  

**Verse 4**  
Night erupts in fireworks’ bloom,  
Cheerful voices fill the room,  
Every sigh becomes a song,  
Together we all belong.  

**Chorus (repeat)**  
Joy erupts, we rise as one,  
Celebration’s fire’s never done,  
Raise your voice, let spirits free,  
Together we make the world glee.
