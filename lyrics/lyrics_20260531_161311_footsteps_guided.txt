**Verse 1**  
Through midnight fog my steps began to sway,  
A faint light flickered, a compass of my mind.  
Guided by a voice that came to stay,  
I followed where the silver shadows aligned.  

**Verse 2**  
A winding river carved the path I tread,  
Each ripple sang a story, soft and clear.  
With every footfall, doubts were softly shed,  
And hope rose up, dissolving doubt and fear.  

**Verse 3**  
I traced the footprints carved by those before,  
Their echoes humming like a timeless chord.  
Their whispers taught me what I still adore—  
That love can walk when hearts are gently heard.  

**Verse 4**  
Now sunrise paints the horizon with gold,  
Each step I take is a promise I keep.  
The guidance that I’ve held, forever bold,  
Lights the road where endless dreams still sweep.  

**Chorus**  
Guided by the rhythm of the heart,  
Footsteps echo, marching through the night.  
With every stride we’re never far apart,  
The path unfolds, a luminous light.
