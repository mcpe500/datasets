**Verse 1**  
Morning light awakens the sparrow’s wing,  
He lifts from the branch, a quiet whisper to sing.  
Feathers catch the breeze, the world slips below,  
A sky‑borne dancer with nothing to show.  

**Verse 2**  
The hawk circles high, a sentinel in blue,  
Eyes sharp as sunrise, the horizon in view.  
Wind carries his song, a spiral of grace,  
Each beat of his heart a familiar place.  

**Verse 3**  
Over mountain peaks, the eagle dare to soar,  
Clouds are his companions, the storm a warm door.  
He rides the thermals, a lazy carousel,  
Leaving the earth to whisper and swell.  

**Verse 4**  
At dusk the starlings turn, a murmuring tide,  
Swirling in patterns the night can’t hide.  
They chase the last light, then fold into night,  
A fleeting fireworks of feathers and flight.  

**Chorus**  
We’re all like birds when we dare to rise,  
Chasing the wind beneath open skies.  
Let the horizon pull us, the heart be our wing,  
In every sunrise, we learn to sing.
