**Verse 1**  
In the hush of a midnight room, a note lingers low,  
A melody the heart knows, but the lips never flow.  
It hides in the rustle of pages, the sigh of the rain,  
A song that waits in shadows, forever in refrain.  

**Verse 2**  
Streets echo with voices, each humming their own tune,  
Yet many remain silent, like a rose that never blooms.  
We carry verses inside us, untold and unspoken,  
Quiet chords that shimmer, forever left unbroken.  

**Verse 3**  
When the world leans close and the moment feels just right,  
The heart lifts its veil, lets the chorus take its flight.  
A whisper turns to thunder, a sigh becomes a song,  
The unsung finds its stage, and we all sing along.  

**Verse 4**  
Now the night is softer, the echo starts to fade,  
But the songs we finally voiced leave a lasting shade.  
In the quiet afterglow, new verses start to grow,  
A circle of the sung and unsung, forever to and fro.  

**Chorus**  
Oh, the songs unsung, they linger in the breeze,  
A promise of the heart, a hopeful, endless tease.  
Let the melody rise, let the silence break apart,  
For every unsung song finds its place in the dark.
