Here are five well‑known dinosaur genera (types):

1. **Tyrannosaurus rex** – a large, bipedal carnivore known for its powerful bite.  
2. **Triceratops** – a horned, quadrupedal herbivore with a distinctive frill.  
3. **Velociraptor** – a small, agile, feathered dromaeosaurid often depicted as a swift hunter.  
4. **Stegosaurus** – a armored, herbivorous dinosaur recognized by the row of large plates along its back.  
5. **Brachiosaurus** – a giant, long‑necked sauropod that browsed on high vegetation.
