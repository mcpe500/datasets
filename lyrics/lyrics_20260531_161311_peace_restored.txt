**Verse 1**  
The thunder rolls, the skies are grey,  
Broken hearts in disarray.  
But a whisper rides on sunrise wind,  
A promise that the war will end.  

**Verse 2**  
Silence finds the shattered streets,  
Where once the echoes drummed defeat.  
Now children laugh and lovers hold,  
The story of a trust retold.  

**Verse 3**  
Mountains bow, the rivers calm,  
The world embraces a gentle calm.  
No more the clang of steel on steel,  
Only the song of healing zeal.  

**Verse 4**  
Through the night we tread the path,  
Guided by a shared, bright graph.  
Together we rise, together we heal,  
In the light of peace, our lives are real.  

**Chorus**  
Peace restored, the dawn is bright,  
Hearts aligned, darkness turns to light.  
We sing as one, the world reborn,  
In every soul, a future sworn.  

*(Repeat chorus for emphasis if desired.)*
