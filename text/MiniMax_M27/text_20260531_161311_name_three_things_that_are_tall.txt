Here are three examples of things that are tall:

1. **Skyscrapers** – massive office towers or residential buildings that can rise hundreds of meters into the sky.  
2. **Giraffes** – the world’s tallest living land animals, with adult males reaching about 18 feet (5.5 meters) in height.  
3. **Redwood trees** – especially coast redwoods and giant sequoias, which can grow over 300 feet (≈ 90 meters) tall.
