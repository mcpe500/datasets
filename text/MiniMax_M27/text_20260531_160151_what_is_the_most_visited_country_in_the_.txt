**France** is currently the world’s most‑visited country.

- **2023 UNWTO data:** France received around **80 million international tourist arrivals** in 2023, making it the top destination globally.  
- The next‑closest countries were **Spain (~70 million), the United States (~67 million), Italy (~57 million), and Turkey (~55 million).**  
- These figures represent a strong rebound from the COVID‑19 dip (2020–2021) and are close to the pre‑pandemic record of 94 million in 2019.

*Source:* UN World Tourism Organization (UNWTO), “World Tourism Barometer” (January 2024 edition).
