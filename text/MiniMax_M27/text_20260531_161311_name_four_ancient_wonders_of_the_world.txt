Here are four of the original Seven Wonders of the Ancient World:

1. **The Great Pyramid of Giza** – Egypt (c. 2580 BC)  
2. **The Hanging Gardens of Babylon** – Iraq (c. 600 BC)  
3. **The Statue of Zeus at Olympia** – Greece (c. 435 BC)  
4. **The Temple of Artemis at Ephesus** – Turkey (c. 550 BC)  

These are among the most celebrated ancient constructions that survived (or are recorded) in classical antiquity.
