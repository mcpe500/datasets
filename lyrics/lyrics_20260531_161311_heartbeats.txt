**Verse 1**  
When the night falls soft and the city hums low,  
I hear the echo of a drum that won’t let go.  
A steady thump‑thump in the silence of my chest,  
A secret pulse that whispers, “You’re not like the rest.”  

**Verse 2**  
We met in a café, the coffee steam in the air,  
Your smile lit the room, and my heart beat a new prayer.  
Two strangers turned to rhythm, a duet of our veins,  
Every breath we shared felt like soft, steady rain.  

**Verse 3**  
Now the world can spin, the world can shake and roar,  
But my heart keeps its tempo, anchored to your core.  
Through stormy seas and midnight skies, the beat stays true,  
A metronome of love that only grows anew.  

**Verse 4**  
When the sunrise paints the horizon, gold and bright,  
Our heartbeats sync like lanterns in the fading night.  
No distance can outrun the pulse that binds us tight,  
In every silent moment, love sings its own light.  

**Chorus**  
Heartbeats, heartbeats, echoing in the dark,  
Two souls in sync, lighting up the spark.  
Feel the rhythm, feel the fire, feel it rise,  
In every beat, our love never dies.
