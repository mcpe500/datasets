The last guest at the inn left a single black feather on the bedside table. When the innkeeper picked it up, the feather whispered, “Don’t look behind the door,” and the hallway lights flickered out. He turned anyway. The door opened onto an endless, empty hallway of mirrors—each reflection wearing his own terrified face. He tried to close the door, but the mirrors kept multiplying, each one echoing his own scream. The inn is quiet now; the feather rests on the table, waiting for the next night.
