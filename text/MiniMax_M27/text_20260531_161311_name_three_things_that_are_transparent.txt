Here are three examples of things that are transparent:

1. **Glass** – windowpanes, drinking glasses, and lenses are typically clear and let light pass through.
2. **Water** – a body of water or a glass of water appears clear, allowing you to see objects beneath the surface.
3. **Clear plastic (e.g., acrylic or polycarbonate)** – sheets and containers made from these materials are transparent and are used for everything from packaging to protective shields.
