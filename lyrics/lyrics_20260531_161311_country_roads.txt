**Verse 1**  
The dust hangs gold on the old gravel lane,  
A maple whispers where the wind has been.  
Morning light rolls slow across the field,  
And the heart finds peace where the dirt will heal.  

**Verse 2**  
Echoes of laughter drift from the barn,  
A rusted fence holds stories near and far.  
The sky paints amber as the sun goes down,  
Each mile draws closer to a quiet town.  

**Verse 3**  
The night sky flickers with firefly spark,  
A chorus of crickets lights up the dark.  
Worn wooden benches creak beneath the weight,  
Of wandering souls who never stay.  

**Verse 4**  
A rusted sign reads “Turn Back Home” in moss,  
The road unfolds like a soft, gentle gloss.  
Every step feels like an old‑time hymn,  
The world outside fades, the heart runs thin.  

**Chorus**  
Take me down the country roads,  
Where the rivers hum and the pines stand tall.  
Let the sunrise paint my soul,  
And the dirt beneath my feet recall.  
Take me home on those winding lanes,  
Where the world is simple and love remains.
