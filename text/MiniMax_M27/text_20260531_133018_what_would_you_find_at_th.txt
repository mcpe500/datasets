The answer depends on whether you’re looking at the science of rainbows or the folklore that’s grown up around them.

### Scientific view
A rainbow isn’t a physical object that sits in the sky; it’s an **optical phenomenon** that appears when sunlight is refracted, reflected, and dispersed by countless water droplets. Because each observer sees the rainbow from a slightly different angle, the “end” of the arc is an illusion—it moves with you and isn’t a fixed spot you can walk to. In other words, you can’t actually *reach* the end of a rainbow, because it’s not a place you can stand on.

### Folk‑lore and cultural legend
In many cultures the end of a rainbow is a place of hidden treasure or wishes:

| Tradition | What’s said to be at the rainbow’s end |
|-----------|------------------------------------------|
| **Irish myth** | A **pot of gold** guarded by a leprechaun. The idea comes from the belief that the rainbow points the way to where a leprechaun has buried his wealth. |
| **Children’s stories** | Often promise a **wish** or a magical surprise (e.g., a sparkling garden, a hidden kingdom). |
| **Japanese folklore** | Some legends speak of a **bridge** to the spirit world, where the soul can cross over. |
| **Native‑American tales** | The rainbow can be a **path** guiding travelers to a place of peace or a new beginning. |
| **Modern pop‑culture** | A “rainbow’s end” is a metaphor for an **ideal outcome**—like finding your perfect job, a dream home, or true love. |

### Bottom line
- **Physically:** There’s nothing at the end of a rainbow because the rainbow itself is just light and water droplets, not a tangible object with a fixed location.  
- **Symbolically:** The end of a rainbow is a **pot of gold**, a place where wishes come true, or any other treasure the storyteller chooses to imagine.

If you’re after the gold, you might have better luck chasing a leprechaun than a rainbow! If you’re curious about the optics, just remember that the rainbow is always a little ahead of you—no matter where you stand. 🌈✨
