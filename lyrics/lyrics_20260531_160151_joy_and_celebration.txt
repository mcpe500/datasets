**Verse 1**  
Morning light spills golden on the street,  
Laughter rises, a rhythm in the air.  
We dance like fireflies, care‑free and sweet,  
Every heartbeat echoes, “Welcome there!”  

**Verse 2**  
Friends gather close, the world feels brand‑new,  
Singing voices blend like sunrise hues.  
We share a cup of warm, sweet brew,  
And let the night turn bright as sunrise views.  

**Verse 3**  
Music swells, the drums keep time,  
A chorus of smiles, a universal rhyme.  
We shout out loud, “All’s well and fine!”  
With every step we make, the stars align.  

**Verse 4**  
From rooftops down to bustling lanes,  
The city hums with joy’s refrain.  
Every moment a fireworks chain,  
Celebration lives in sun and rain.  

**Chorus**  
Raise your voice, let the echo shine,  
Joy is a melody, celebration’s line.  
We’re dancing free, the world’s our stage,  
Together we shine, for ever in this age.
