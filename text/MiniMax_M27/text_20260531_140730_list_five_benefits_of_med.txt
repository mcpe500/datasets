**Five benefits of meditation**

- **Stress reduction** – Regular practice lowers cortisol levels and helps the body relax, which can lessen the physical and emotional impact of everyday stressors.  
- **Improved focus and attention** – Meditation trains the mind to stay present, sharpening concentration and making it easier to sustain attention on tasks.  
- **Greater emotional balance** – By fostering mindfulness, meditation can increase positive emotions, reduce symptoms of anxiety and depression, and promote a more stable mood.  
- **Enhanced self‑awareness** – Paying attention to thoughts, feelings, and bodily sensations without judgment helps you recognize patterns, strengths, and areas for personal growth.  
- **Better sleep quality** – Calming the mind and relaxing the nervous system can make it easier to fall asleep, stay asleep, and wake feeling refreshed.
