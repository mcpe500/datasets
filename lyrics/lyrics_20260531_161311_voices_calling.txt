**Verse 1**  
In the hush before the sunrise, a whisper rides the breeze,  
a distant, gentle murmur that drifts through ancient trees.  
It pulls the restless heartstrings, like a lantern in the night,  
a beckoning that’s familiar, a softly glowing light.  

**Verse 2**  
Through the corridors of midnight, the echo finds its way,  
a chorus of the forgotten, the ones who’ve gone astray.  
Their stories twist and turn, a tapestry of sighs,  
calling me to follow where the secret river lies.  

**Verse 3**  
Each rustle of the curtain, each creak upon the floor,  
reminds me of the cadence that’s hidden in the core.  
The voices call in colors, in riddles made of sound,  
a melody that guides me where the lost are finally found.  

**Verse 4**  
When the world is filled with chatter, I pause to hear that call,  
the steady, hopeful rhythm that refuses to let fall.  
It speaks of brighter mornings, of truths we long to know,  
the promise of a sunrise where the spirits’ whispers flow.  

**Chorus**  
Voices calling, calling me home,  
through the shadows, through the dawn.  
In the quiet, in the roar,  
they lead me to that distant shore.
