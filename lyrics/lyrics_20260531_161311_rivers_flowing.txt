**Verse 1**  
Morning mist curls over the valley’s edge,  
as silver ribbons twist and glide.  
The river whispers secrets, cold and clear,  
a song that starts where mountains meet the tide.  

**Verse 2**  
It winds through forests, brushing ancient stones,  
each ripple a memory it carries downstream.  
The water hums of rain that fell on high,  
and every turn reveals a fleeting dream.  

**Verse 3**  
Fields of gold bow as the current passes,  
the banks hold fast, yet yield to its sigh.  
It mirrors sky‑blue thoughts of wandering clouds,  
while life along its banks breathes low and high.  

**Verse 4**  
Onward, onward, past towns and quiet towns,  
the river knows no pause, no final end—  
until it meets the vast, eternal sea,  
where all the flows become one timeless blend.  

**Chorus**  
Rivers flowing, ever moving, ever free,  
carrying the world in gentle, endless streams.  
Rivers flowing, teaching us to let things be,  
and finding our own path in the ever‑shining gleam.
