**Verse 1**  
In the quiet hush of morning light,  
I plant a seed of dreams in the mind’s soil,  
Each thought a blossom, soft and bright,  
Nurtured by curiosity’s gentle toil.  

**Verse 2**  
We walk the pathways of our ideas,  
Roses of reason, thorns of doubt,  
With every breeze, the memory veers,  
And echoes of the past are all about.  

**Verse 3**  
Watered by wonder, stems rise high,  
Branches of stories intertwine,  
The garden breathes beneath the sky,  
A sanctuary where the soul can dine.  

**Verse 4**  
When seasons shift and colors fade,  
We prune the weary, keep the true,  
In the garden where the heart is made,  
The roots of insight stay anew.  

**Chorus**  
Oh, gardens of thought, blooming bright,  
We wander, we grow, we shine,  
In every mind a garden’s light,  
A melody of forever’s design.
