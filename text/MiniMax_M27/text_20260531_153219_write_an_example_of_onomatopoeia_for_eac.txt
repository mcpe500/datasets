**A – Z Onomatopoeia Examples**

- **A** – *Achoo* (the sound of a sneeze)  
- **B** – *Bang* (an explosion or sudden hit)  
- **C** – *Clack* (the click of a typewriter key)  
- **D** – *Drip* (a water drop falling)  
- **E** – *Eek* (a startled mouse’s high‑pitched scream)  
- **F** – *Fizz* (the bubbling of carbonated drink)  
- **G** – *Gulp* (swallowing a drink quickly)  
- **H** – *Hiss* (steam or a snake’s sound)  
- **I** – *Ick* (an expression of disgust)  
- **J** – *Jingle* (the ringing of small bells)  
- **K** – *Knock* (a knock on a door)  
- **L** – *Lap* (water splashing against the shore)  
- **M** – *Moo* (a cow’s low‑pitch call)  
- **N** – *Neigh* (a horse’s whinny)  
- **O** – *Oink* (a pig’s grunt)  
- **P** – *Pop* (a balloon bursting)  
- **Q** – *Quack* (a duck’s call)  
- **R** – *Rumble* (distant thunder or an engine’s low roar)  
- **S** – *Sizzle* (food frying in a pan)  
- **T** – *Thud* (a heavy object hitting the ground)  
- **U** – *Uff* (a grunt or sigh of effort)  
- **V** – *Vroom* (the revving of an engine)  
- **W** – *Wham* (a sudden, forceful impact)  
- **X** – *Xylophonic* (the bright “ting‑tang” of a xylophone)  
- **Y** – *Yap* (a small dog’s sharp bark)  
- **Z** – *Zap* (the crackle of an electric spark)
