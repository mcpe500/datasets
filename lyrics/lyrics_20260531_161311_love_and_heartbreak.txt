**Verse 1**  
The night we met, the stars seemed to lean in,  
Your smile lit the dark like a lantern in rain.  
We danced in the glow of a promise so bright,  
Believing forever would hold us tight.  

**Verse 2**  
Days turned to weeks, the whispers grew louder,  
Your hand in mine felt like a soft summer shower.  
But shadows crept in where the laughter once lived,  
And the words we once spoke began to mis‑give.  

**Verse 3**  
A single cold word, a careless goodbye,  
The echo of love fell like ash from the sky.  
I held onto memories, fragile as glass,  
And watched each bright fragment turn to ash.  

**Verse 4**  
Now the streets feel empty, the coffee’s too bitter,  
Your scent lingers still in the edge of the city.  
I breathe in the pain, let it carve out a new song,  
A heart that can love again, though it’s bruised and worn.  

**Chorus**  
Oh love, you’re a storm that can break and restore,  
You’ll crumble the walls I built high on your shore.  
Heartbreak may linger, but sunrise will call,  
I’ll rise from the ashes, and love will stand tall.
