**Verse 1**  
When the sky turns gray and the winds turn cold,  
I dig my heels in, I refuse to fold.  
Every thunder rolls, I stand taller still,  
A burning fire inside, my stubborn will.  

**Verse 2**  
Mountains rise where the path seems lost,  
I climb the rocks, no matter the cost.  
Each step a heartbeat, each breath a vow,  
I’ll carve my way, I’ll show them how.  

**Verse 3**  
Dreams may shatter like glass on the floor,  
But I gather the pieces and build once more.  
Broken pieces become a brand‑new song,  
A chorus of courage that proves me strong.  

**Verse 4**  
When night feels endless and hope feels thin,  
I light a spark from the darkness within.  
Resilience flows like a river that never ends,  
With every trial, my spirit mends.  

**Chorus**  
I’m the fire that never fades, the stone that won’t break,  
With every storm I rise, a force I create.  
Strength is the rhythm, resilience the rhyme,  
Together we’re unstoppable, we’re beating the time.
