**Verse 1**  
In the hush of twilight, I feel your pulse,  
A soft echo that drifts across the void,  
Two hearts whisper, drawing a hidden line,  
Bound by the glow of a promise unseen.  

**Verse 2**  
Even when mountains rise and oceans spread,  
Your rhythm tides inside my chest and sings,  
Each breath we share weaves a golden thread,  
Our love a bridge that the world can’t break.  

**Verse 3**  
Through stormy skies, our fire stays alight,  
When doubts creep in, our souls hold tight,  
Two voices rise in a chorus of trust,  
Together we climb, no stone left unturned.  

**Verse 4**  
Seasons turn, yet our cadence stays the same,  
Time’s hands may wander, but hearts never part,  
Bound by a vow written in starlight,  
We dance in the dark, forever in sync.  

**Chorus**  
Hearts connected, beating as one,  
Our pulse a melody, never undone,  
Across any distance, love’s endless song,  
Together we shine, forever as one.
