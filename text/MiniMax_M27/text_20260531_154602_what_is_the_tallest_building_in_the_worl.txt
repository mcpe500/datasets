**Burj Khalifa** (Dubai, United Arab Emirates)

- **Height:** 828 meters (2,717 feet)  
- **Floors:** 163 (occupied) + 46 maintenance/staff levels  
- **Completed:** January 2010  
- **Architectural firm:** Skidmore, Owings & Merrill (Adrian Smith)  
- **Notable facts:**  
  - Holds the record for the tallest building in the world since its opening.  
  - Its spire alone is about 244 m tall, part of the total height.  
  - Designed with a “buttressed core” structural system that allows the building to stay stable under high winds.  

No other completed building has surpassed the Burj Khalifa’s height as of early 2026. (The Jeddah Tower in Saudi Arabia, once planned to exceed 1,000 m, is still under construction.)
