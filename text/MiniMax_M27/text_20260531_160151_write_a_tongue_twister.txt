Here’s a classic‑style tongue‑twister for you:

**“Sally sells sea‑shells by the sea‑shore,  
while Sue sells sizzling, salty, sweet, and sour sea‑snacks,  
so the seagulls swiftly swirl, snatch, and sip the salty sea‑spray,  
and Susie’s sister sews silky, shimmering sea‑shell skirts.”**  

Or, if you’d like a fresh one, try this all‑iteration‑packed challenge:

**“Freddy flips fresh, fluffy, frosted French‑fry fritters,  
fancying the fiery, fragrant, fragrant fennel‑filled fizzy fizz,  
while Frankie, the fearless, fast‑flying firefighter,  
floods the frantic, flickering flames with frantic, frothy foam.”**  

Give them a try—you’ll be tripping over those tricky t’s and s’s in no time!
