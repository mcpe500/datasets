**[Verse 1]**  
Neon lights flicker, the night becomes a stage,  
The bass drum pulses, a heart that won’t disengage.  
Feet tap to the beat, the world spins in time,  
Our bodies are instruments, dancing in perfect rhyme.  

**[Verse 2]**  
Every step we take leaves a story on the floor,  
The echo of the drum invites us to explore.  
We sway like waves, the music lifts us high,  
A symphony of movement painted across the sky.  

**[Verse 3]**  
In the heat of the moment the rhythm’s deep inside,  
Our hearts start to sync, beating side by side.  
We spin and we twirl, let the music lead the way,  
The world fades away, only the beat stays.  

**[Verse 4]**  
The crowd becomes one, a sea of synchronized flow,  
Each pulse is a thread that binds us to the glow.  
From the first note to the last, we dance without fear,  
Let the rhythm guide us, our spirits stay clear.  

**[Chorus]**  
Feel the rhythm, feel the beat, it moves through the night,  
Our bodies swing, our souls ignite.  
We rise and fall, like waves upon the shore,  
Dance is the language we all adore.
