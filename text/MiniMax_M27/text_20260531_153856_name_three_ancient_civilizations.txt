Here are three well‑known ancient civilizations:

1. **Mesopotamia** – The region between the Tigris and Euphrates rivers (modern‑day Iraq) where the Sumerians, Akkadians, Babylonians, and Assyrians built city‑states, developed writing (cuneiform), codified laws (e.g., the Code of Hammurabi), and created early forms of mathematics, astronomy, and literature.

2. **Ancient Egypt** – Flourishing along the Nile River for millennia, this civilization is famed for its monumental architecture (pyramids, temples), hieroglyphic writing, a sophisticated polytheistic religion, centralized pharaonic rule, and achievements in medicine, agriculture, and art.

3. **Indus Valley (Harappan) Civilization** – Centered in the northwestern Indian subcontinent (modern Pakistan and northwest India), it is known for its well‑planned cities (Mohenjo‑daro, Harappa), standardized weights and measures, a script that remains undeciphered, and advanced drainage and water‑management systems.
