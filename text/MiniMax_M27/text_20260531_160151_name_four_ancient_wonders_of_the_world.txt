Here are four of the most famous **Seven Wonders of the Ancient World**:

1. **Great Pyramid of Giza** – The only wonder that still stands today, built for the Egyptian pharaoh Khufu (c. 2580 BC).  
2. **Hanging Gardens of Babylon** – A tiered, lush garden said to have been constructed in the ancient city of Babylon (modern‑day Iraq) around 600 BC.  
3. **Statue of Zeus at Olympia** – A giant gold‑ and ivory‑covered statue of the Greek god Zeus, created by the sculptor Phidias around 435 BC in the Temple of Zeus at Olympia.  
4. **Mausoleum at Halicarnassus** – A large, ornate tomb built for the Carian ruler Mausolus (c. 353 BC) in what is now Bodrum, Turkey.  

These four were celebrated in antiquity for their extraordinary size, beauty, and engineering achievements.
