Here are three well‑known ancient civilizations:

1. **Ancient Egypt** – famed for its pharaohs, pyramids, hieroglyphic writing, and the Nile River‑based culture that flourished for millennia.
2. **Mesopotamia** – the “land between rivers” (modern Iraq) where the Sumerians, Akkadians, Babylonians, and Assyrians built city‑states, developed cuneiform writing, and created early law codes.
3. **Indus Valley (Harappan) Civilization** – a Bronze‑Age culture in present‑day Pakistan and northwest India, noted for its sophisticated city planning, standardized weights and measures, and the undeciphered script.
