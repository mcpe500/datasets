The **cheetah** (*Acinonyx jubatus*) holds the record as the fastest land animal. It can reach speeds of about **70 – 75 mph (112 – 120 km/h)** in short bursts while hunting.  

For comparison, other quick land mammals include:

| Animal | Top speed (approx.) |
|--------|--------------------|
| Pronghorn | 55 mph (88 km/h) |
| Springbok | 55 mph (88 km/h) |
| Wildebeest | 50 mph (80 km/h) |
| Lion | 50 mph (80 km/h) |
| Quarter Horse | 55 mph (88 km/h) |

The cheetah’s acceleration and speed are adaptations for catching swift prey in open savannas, making it the undisputed speed champion on land.
