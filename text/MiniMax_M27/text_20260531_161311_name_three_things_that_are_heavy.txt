Here are three examples of heavy objects:

1. **An adult African elephant** – can weigh up to 6 tons (≈5,400 kg).  
2. **A grand piano** – a full‑size concert grand can weigh around 500 kg (≈1,100 lb).  
3. **A loaded 20‑foot shipping container** – when filled with cargo it often exceeds 20 tons (≈20,000 kg).
