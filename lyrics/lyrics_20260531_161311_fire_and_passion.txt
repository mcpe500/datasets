**Verse 1**  
Midnight whispers stir the night,  
Embers flicker, lighting the way,  
Our hearts beat with a crimson tide,  
A love that burns beyond all stay.  

**Verse 2**  
The world may try to douse our blaze,  
Yet we rise from the ash, amazed,  
Each breath a spark, each sigh a fuse,  
Our love’s inferno refuses to lose.  

**Verse 3**  
We walk through valleys of molten steel,  
Clutching each other's trembling zeal,  
Heat between us, a sacred dance,  
A symphony of flames that grants us a chance.  

**Verse 4**  
When storm clouds brew we won’t retreat,  
Our fire is a beacon, bright and sweet,  
Together we blaze, untamed and free,  
Forever burning, ever boundless sea.  

**Chorus**  
Feel the fire, feel the beat,  
It pulses, roars, a constant heat,  
Burn with me, ignite the night,  
Our love will blaze forever bright.
