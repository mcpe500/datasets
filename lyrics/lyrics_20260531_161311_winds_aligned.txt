**Verse 1**  
Morning breezes drift from hills, converge, and sway,  
They find a rhythm, a chorus in the grey,  
Each gust a whispered note that blends into a song,  
When the winds align, the sky learns a brighter tone.  

**Verse 2**  
Through bustling streets the leaves lift, light as sighs,  
They carry dreams from wanderers, old and wise,  
Unseen threads of wind weave heart to trembling heart,  
When the winds align, they stitch the world apart.  

**Verse 3**  
Across the mountain passes, gusts in perfect line,  
Carve a path of song where eagles start to climb,  
A single breath of air becomes a rising tide,  
When the winds align, the mountains start to glide.  

**Verse 4**  
At twilight’s edge the breezes pause, hold their breath,  
The world leans in to listen, quiet as a wreath,  
Then softest sighs resume the ancient, steadfast score,  
When the winds align, we feel forevermore.  

**Chorus**  
When the winds align, the world finds its flow,  
Our spirits rise, the shadows fade below,  
Together we glide on unseen, endless streams,  
In perfect harmony, we chase the dream.
