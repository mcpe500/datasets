**Verse 1**  
The playground swings sway in golden light,  
Your giggles rose like ribbons in the air.  
Their echo chased the clouds, pure and bright,  
A memory that lingered everywhere.  

**Verse 2**  
Down cobblestone alleys, friends gathered late,  
Their laughter bounced off brick, a lively chant.  
Each burst of joy created a new slate,  
A melody that echoed, forever flaunt.  

**Verse 3**  
Grandma’s kitchen still smells of fresh bread,  
Her chuckle lingered like a warm refrain.  
Even when the night fell and silence spread,  
That echo glowed, turning sorrow to gain.  

**Verse 4**  
Now children sprint through sunrise meadows,  
Their laughter rings, a chorus in the breeze.  
Across the hills, it forever follows,  
A timeless song that sets our hearts at ease.  

**Chorus**  
Echoes of laughter, bright and true,  
Travel through years, across every sea.  
In every heart, they spark anew,  
A symphony that sings of you and me.
