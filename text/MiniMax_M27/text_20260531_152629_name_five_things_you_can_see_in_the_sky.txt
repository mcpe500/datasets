Sure! Here are five common things you can see in the sky:

1. **Sun** – the bright star that provides daylight.  
2. **Moon** – especially visible at night (and sometimes during the day).  
3. **Stars** – tiny points of light that become visible after sunset.  
4. **Clouds** – water droplets or ice crystals that drift across the sky in various shapes.  
5. **Birds (or flying insects)** – creatures that soar and glide through the air.
