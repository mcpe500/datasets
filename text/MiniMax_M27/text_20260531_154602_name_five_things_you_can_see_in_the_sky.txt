Here are five things you might see when you look up at the sky:

1. **Sun** – the bright star that provides daylight.  
2. **Moon** – Earth’s natural satellite, often visible at night (and sometimes during the day).  
3. **Clouds** – masses of water droplets or ice crystals that drift across the sky.  
4. **Stars** – countless distant points of light that appear after sunset.  
5. **Birds** – flying creatures that often pass overhead, especially at dawn and dusk.
