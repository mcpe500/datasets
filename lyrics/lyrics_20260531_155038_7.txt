**Verse 1**  
There’s a fire inside my chest, a flicker, a spark,  
It whispers of horizons I’ve never seen in the dark.  
I chase the distant glow where the sunrise meets the sea,  
With every step I take, the world leans closer to me.  

**Verse 2**  
Mountains stand in shadows, but I hear their ancient call,  
Each cliff a stepping stone, each valley a newborn thrall.  
I write my name on clouds, let the wind rewrite the line,  
In the garden of my mind, the impossible turns fine.  

**Verse 3**  
Night may pull the curtain, yet the stars still paint the sky,  
I hold onto the chorus of the song that won’t say goodbye.  
Every stumble is a note, every scar a deeper chord,  
I’m weaving my own anthem, a story to be stored.  

**Verse 4**  
Future is a canvas, blank and waiting for my hand,  
I’ll paint it bold and bright, with colors I command.  
Dreams are more than wishes; they’re the wings I let unfurl,  
So I rise, I reach, I claim my place in this world.  

**Chorus**  
We’re the dreamers, forever chasing sunrise,  
Our aspirations lift us past the doubts that rise.  
With every heartbeat, the melody grows stronger,  
Together we’ll compose a world where we belong longer.
