Here are four commonly recognized oceanic zones (from the surface down):

1. **Epipelagic (Sunlight) Zone** – Extends from the surface to about 200 m (≈ 650 ft). Ample sunlight allows photosynthesis; the most biologically productive region.

2. **Mesopelagic (Twilight) Zone** – From ~200 m down to about 1,000 m (≈ 3,300 ft). Light is dim; many organisms migrate vertically daily.

3. **Bathypelagic (Midnight) Zone** – From ~1,000 m to about 4,000 m (≈ 13,000 ft). No sunlight, constant cold, and high pressure.

4. **Abyssopelagic (Abyssal) Zone** – From ~4,000 m down to the ocean floor at about 6,000 m (≈ 20,000 ft). Extremely cold, nearly pitch‑black, and under immense pressure. (The deepest, “hadal” zone, is sometimes listed as a separate category for the deepest trenches.)
