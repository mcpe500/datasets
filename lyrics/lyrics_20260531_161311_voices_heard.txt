**Verse 1**  
Morning drifts through the cracked‑open window,  
A faint murmur rides the cool breeze,  
Echoes of yesterday’s laughter and sorrow,  
A chorus of stories that wander and seize.  

**Verse 2**  
Inside the mind, a quiet conversation,  
Footsteps of thought that tap on the brain,  
Voices that dance between hope and hesitation,  
Guiding the heart through sunshine and rain.  

**Verse 3**  
Across the street, a stranger’s sigh,  
His murmur folds into the city’s hum,  
Words on the wind swirl, touch, and fly,  
A shared sigh becomes a silent drum.  

**Verse 4**  
When night falls, the world grows still,  
The distant hum becomes a lullaby,  
Each voice a star that steadies the will,  
Together they paint the sky in a sigh.  

**Chorus**  
In every silence, the voices are heard,  
A tapestry woven of whispers and wonder,  
They echo our name, they echo the world,  
A symphony of souls we hear from under.
