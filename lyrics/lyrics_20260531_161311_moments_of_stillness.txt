**Verse 1**  
Morning dew clings to the quiet air,  
The sunrise whispers, the world holds its breath,  
A gentle hush drapes the golden hour,  
In that pause my thoughts find calm.  

**Chorus**  
In the stillness we find a gentle light,  
Silent moments like a soft, steady tide,  
The world pauses and our hearts take flight,  
In quiet breaths we see the truth.
