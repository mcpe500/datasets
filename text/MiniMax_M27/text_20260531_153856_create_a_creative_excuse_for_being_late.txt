**Tips for using these excuses:**
- **Add a grain of truth** – Mention a real event (e.g., a traffic jam, a late train) and then blend in the whimsical twist.
- **Keep it brief** – A punchy story is more memorable than a long saga.
- **Match the tone** – Use a more professional spin for a boss or a more playful one for friends.

If you’re in a situation where the stakes are serious (e.g., a medical appointment or a legal deadline), it’s always safer to be straightforward about why you were late. But for a light‑hearted social slip‑up, one of these might just earn you a chuckle and a little extra leeway! 😊
