**Rain & Renewal – A Short Song Lyric**

**Verse 1**  
Grey clouds gather, the sky sighs low,  
A hush before the storm, a quiet glow.  
Each drop that falls finds thirsty ground,  
Waking seeds that slept underground.  

**Chorus**  
Let the rain wash over me,  
Wash away what used to be.  
In every storm, a chance to grow,  
A brand‑new dawn, a seed set free.  

**Verse 2**

**Verse 3**  
The rooftops whisper, “Come and see,”  
The fresh‑scented air, wild and free.  
The world is hushed, then sings again,

**Chorus**  
Let the rain wash over me,  
Wash away what used to be.

**Verse 4**  
When morning breaks with golden hue,  
The fields exhale a bright‑green view.  
We walk anew, our hearts aligned,  
Renewed by rain, by love, by time.  

**Final Chorus**  
Let the rain wash over us,  
Wash away what was and thus.  
In every storm, a chance to rise,  
A brand‑new dawn, our souls set free.
