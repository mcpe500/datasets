Sure! Here are three common types of clouds:

1. **Cumulus** – puffy, white clouds with flat bases, often seen on fair‑weather days.
2. **Stratus** – low, gray, sheet‑like clouds that can bring overcast skies and light drizzle.
3. **Cirrus** – high, thin, wispy clouds made of ice crystals, usually appearing as feathery streaks in the sky.
