The longest wall in the world is **the Great Wall of China**.  

- **Total length (including all branches and rebuilt sections):** roughly 21,000 km (≈ 13,000 mi).  
- **Longest contiguous stretch:** about 8,850 km (≈ 5,500 mi).  

It stretches from eastern‑central China to the far west, and it’s the most extensive ancient fortification ever built. (Its exact measured length varies depending on how one counts fragmented or restored sections, but it consistently holds the record as the world’s longest wall.)
