The most populous **city‑wide** (metropolitan) area in the world is **Tokyo, Japan**.  

- **Tokyo’s metropolitan area (Tokyo‑Yokohama‑Kawasaki)** is home to roughly **37 million people** (2023 estimate).  
- If you’re looking at only the “city‑proper” population (the administrative city limits), the current leader is **Delhi, India**, with about **30 million** residents in its municipal boundaries.  

So, depending on whether you count the whole metro region or just the official city limits, the answer is either **Tokyo** (metro) or **Delhi** (city‑proper).
