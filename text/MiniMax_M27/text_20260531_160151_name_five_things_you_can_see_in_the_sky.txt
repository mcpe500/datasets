Sure! Here are five things you can commonly see in the sky:

1. **Sun** – the bright star at the center of our solar system.  
2. **Moon** – Earth’s natural satellite, often visible at night (and sometimes during the day).  
3. **Clouds** – masses of water droplets or ice crystals suspended in the atmosphere.  
4. **Stars** – distant suns that appear as tiny points of light, especially on clear nights.  
5. **Birds** – various species of birds flying through the air.
