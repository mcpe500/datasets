**Forest Breathing**

**Verse 1**  
In the hush of dawn the pines exhale,  
A soft sigh of mist that curls and trails.  
Their needles sip the sunrise gold,  
As roots draw deep from the earthen hold.

**Chorus**  
Breathe with the forest, feel the rise,  
In every leaf a whispered lullaby.  
The world breathes in, the green exhales,  
A living rhythm that never fails.

**Verse 2**  
When noon‑light paints the canopy in jade,  
The oaks inhale the heat they’ve made.  
A chorus of rustling wings takes flight,  
While the soil drinks the day’s warm light.

**Chorus**  
Breathe with the forest, feel the rise,  
In every leaf a whispered lullaby.  
The world breathes in, the green exhales,  
A living rhythm that never fails.

**Verse 3**  
At dusk the maples whisper low,  
A sigh that bends the shadows’ flow.  
The night drapes over bark and root,  
A quiet breath that makes the forest mute.

**Chorus**  
Breathe with the forest, feel the rise,  
In every leaf a whispered lullaby.  
The world breathes in, the green exhales,  
A living rhythm that never fails.

**Verse 4**  
When winter’s hush drapes ice on pine,  
The silent wood draws in the cold confined.  
But deep beneath the frozen ground,  
A steady pulse of life is found.

**Chorus**  
Breathe with the forest, feel the rise,  
In every leaf a whispered lullaby.  
The world breathes in, the green exhales,  
A living rhythm that never fails.
