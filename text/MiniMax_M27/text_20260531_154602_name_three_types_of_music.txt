Here are three well‑known categories of music:

1. **Classical** – Orchestral and chamber works composed primarily from the Baroque through the Romantic periods (e.g., Mozart, Beethoven, Tchaikovsky).  
2. **Jazz** – A genre rooted in African‑American communities, featuring improvisation, swing or syncopated rhythms, and rich harmonic structures (e.g., Louis Armstrong, Miles Davis, John Coltrane).  
3. **Rock** – A broad style characterized by electric guitars, strong rhythms, and often a four‑beat structure, encompassing everything from classic rock to alternative and heavy metal (e.g., The Beatles, Led Zeppelin, Nirvana).
