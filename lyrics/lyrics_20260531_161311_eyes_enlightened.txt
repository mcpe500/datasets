**Verse 1**  
Morning light slips through the pane,  
A whisper wakes the dormant brain.  
Your eyes, like windows, catch the glow,  
Illuminating all I need to know.  

**Verse 2**  
When shadows fall upon the street,  
Your sight becomes the midnight heat.  
A beacon fires the hidden paths,  
Turning doubts to embered graphs.  

**Verse 3**  
In the quiet of a whispered prayer,  
Your gaze unspools the tangled snare.  
Each glance a key, each blink a door,  
Opening realms I never knew before.  

**Verse 4**  
Now the world is crisp and bright,  
Colors sing in purest light.  
With every breath my soul expands,  
Enlightened by your watchful hands.  

**Chorus**  
Eyes enlightened, shining clear,  
Drawing near the truth we hold dear.  
Through the lens of love we see,  
All that’s bright, forever free.
