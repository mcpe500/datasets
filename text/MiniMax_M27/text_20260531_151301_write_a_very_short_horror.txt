The night watchman heard a soft shuffle behind the old oak door, and a child's laugh echoed from the darkness. He flicked on his flashlight; the beam caught a cracked mirror hanging in the hallway. In the glass, a tiny silhouette stood just behind him, its eyes hollow, its smile wide and wrong. He spun—nothing. The door at the end of the hall creaked open, spilling cold air into the room. When he turned back to the mirror, his own reflection stared back, trembling, as if it were trying to warn him one last time.
