Here are three classic examples of things that tend to be “always improving”:

1. **Technology** – From computing power to consumer gadgets, each generation of hardware and software tends to be faster, smaller, more efficient, and more capable than the last.

2. **Scientific knowledge** – Our understanding of the natural world (physics, biology, climate science, etc.) expands continuously as new experiments, observations, and theories refine or overturn previous ideas.

3. **Standard of living / quality of life** – Over long‑term trends, societies generally gain better access to clean water, healthcare, education, and material comforts, leading to longer life expectancies and higher overall well‑being.
