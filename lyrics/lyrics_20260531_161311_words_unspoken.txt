**Verse 1**  
A whisper caught in the back of my throat,  
Silence folds like paper in the night,  
Each unspoken word a shadowed note,  
Fading into the echo of my mind.  

**Verse 2**  
Between us, the air is heavy with the unseen,  
A sigh that never finds its way to the surface,  
Words unsaid like a bridge that’s never been,  
Leaving us walking on empty rehearsed words.  

**Verse 3**  
I trace the shape of the sentences I swallow,  
They linger like ink on a page never turned,  
And every silence tells a story that’s shallow,  
A truth too shy to be heard.  

**Verse 4**  
In the quiet, my heart beats a restless tune,  
Calling for the moment when my breath can break,  
When the tide of unspoken words will bloom,  
And finally, my voice will wake.  

**Chorus**  
Words unspoken, they linger in the dark,  
A silent storm that stirs the soul,  
They rise like stars, they carve like sparks,  
And wait for the moment they can be told.
