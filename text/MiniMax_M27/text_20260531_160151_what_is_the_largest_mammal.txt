The largest mammal on Earth is the **blue whale** (*Balaenoptera musculus*).  

- **Size** – Adults can reach lengths of up to about 30 m (≈98 ft), and sometimes even longer.  
- **Mass** – They are the heaviest animals ever known, with adults weighing up to roughly 200 tonnes (≈440,000 lb). Their hearts alone can weigh as much as a car.  
- **Distribution** – Blue whales are found in all the world’s oceans, from the Arctic to the Antarctic, usually in deeper offshore waters.  

Because of their massive size, blue whales dwarf even the largest terrestrial mammals (which, for comparison, are about 10 times smaller). They feed primarily on tiny crustaceans called krill, consuming several tons of them each day during feeding season.  

So, when asking “What is the largest mammal?” the answer is unequivocally **the blue whale**.
