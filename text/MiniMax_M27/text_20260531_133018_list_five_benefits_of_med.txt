**Five Benefits of Meditation**

1. **Reduces Stress** – Regular meditation lowers cortisol levels and helps you feel calmer in everyday situations.  
2. **Improves Focus & Concentration** – Practicing mindfulness trains your brain to stay present and ignore distractions.  
3. **Enhances Emotional Well‑Being** – Meditation can increase positivity, reduce anxiety, and promote a more balanced mood.  
4. **Promotes Better Sleep** – By calming the mind and relaxing the body, it can help you fall asleep faster and enjoy deeper rest.  
5. **Supports Physical Health** – Studies show it can lower blood pressure, boost immune function, and decrease inflammation.
