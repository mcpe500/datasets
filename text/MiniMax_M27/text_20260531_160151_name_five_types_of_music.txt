Here are five well‑known genres of music:

1. **Classical** – Orchestral, chamber, and solo instrumental works from the Baroque, Classical, Romantic, and modern periods.  
2. **Rock** – Guitar‑driven, band‑based music with a strong backbeat, often featuring electric guitars and drums.  
3. **Pop** – Short, catchy songs designed for mainstream appeal, typically with a verse‑chorus structure and polished production.  
4. **Jazz** – Improvisation‑focused music that blends swing rhythms, complex harmonies, and a wide range of instrumental textures.  
5. **Hip‑Hop/Rap** – Rhythmic vocal delivery (rapping) over beat‑driven instrumentals, often incorporating sampling and DJ techniques.
