Mount Everest is the world’s tallest mountain when measured by elevation above sea level. It rises to about 8,848 meters (29,029 ft) and lies in the Himalayas on the border between Nepal and the Tibet Autonomous Region of China. 

If you consider “tallest” from base to summit (including the portion underwater), the volcano Mauna Kea in Hawaii would be the tallest, but most references to “the tallest mountain in the world” refer to Everest’s elevation.
