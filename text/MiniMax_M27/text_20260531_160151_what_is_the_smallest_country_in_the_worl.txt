The smallest country in the world is **Vatican City** (Status Civitatis Vaticanae).

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Area** | ~0.44 km² (about 110 acres) |
| **Population** | Roughly 800–900 residents (mostly clergy and Swiss Guards) |
| **Location** | Enclave within Rome, Italy |
| **Government** | Absolute elective monarchy headed by the Pope |
| **Significance** | World‑wide seat of the Roman Catholic Church and the Papacy |

Because it is both the smallest by land area and by permanent population, Vatican City holds the record for the world’s tiniest sovereign state.
