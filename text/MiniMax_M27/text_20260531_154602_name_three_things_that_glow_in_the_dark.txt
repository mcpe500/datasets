Here are three common examples of things that glow in the dark:

1. **Fireflies** – These insects produce a bioluminescent chemical reaction in their abdomens to attract mates.
2. **Glow‑in‑the‑dark paint or stickers** – Specially formulated phosphorescent pigments absorb light and then re‑emit it slowly, creating a visible glow after the lights are turned off.
3. **Certain deep‑sea organisms (e.g., jellyfish, some squid)** – They contain luciferin‑luciferase systems (or similar proteins) that generate light through chemical reactions, allowing them to glow in the darkness of the ocean.
