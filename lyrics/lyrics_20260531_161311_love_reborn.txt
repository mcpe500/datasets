**Verse 1**  
Morning light spills soft through curtains,  
Your smile arrives like sunrise in my chest.  
We thought love had faded, bruised and broken,  
Yet now it blooms anew—a tender quest.  

**Verse 2**  
Dust settles on old photographs, whispers of yesterday,  
But the fire inside never truly died.  
When you took my hand, the world rearranged,  
And love rose from the ashes, side by side.  

**Verse 3**  
Time turned the page, the chapters wrote anew,  
The echo of our promises still rings.  
In every heartbeat, a rhythm of renewal,  
Our love’s reborn, forever singing.  

**Verse 4**  
Night falls, the stars repeat our story,  
Moonlight paints our shadows on the floor.  
Though doubts may whisper, we hold on tightly,  
Love’s forever bright, forevermore.  

**Chorus**  
Love reborn, like sunrise after night,  
A flame that never dims, a gentle light.  
In your eyes the world finds its shore,  
Together we rise, forevermore.
