Here are three broad areas that tend to keep getting better over time:

1. **Technology & Computing** – From faster processors and larger storage to smarter AI assistants, each generation of hardware and software brings greater performance, efficiency, and new capabilities.

2. **Scientific Knowledge & Understanding** – Ongoing research continuously refines our models of physics, biology, climate, and the cosmos, leading to deeper insights and innovative applications.

3. **Medical & Health‑Care Practices** – Advances in diagnostics, treatments, drug development, and preventive care steadily raise life expectancy, reduce mortality, and improve quality of life.
