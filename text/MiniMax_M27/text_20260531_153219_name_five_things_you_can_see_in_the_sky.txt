Here are five common things you might see when you look up at the sky:

1. **Clouds** – Fluffy or wispy collections of water droplets or ice crystals that drift across the sky.
2. **The Sun** – The bright star at the center of our solar system, giving off light and heat during the day.
3. **The Moon** – Earth’s natural satellite, often visible especially at night, sometimes appearing as a crescent or full disk.
4. **Stars** – Distant points of light, many of which are actually distant suns, twinkling in the night sky (and sometimes visible during twilight).
5. **Birds or Aircraft** – Living creatures (birds, bats) or human‑made machines (airplanes, drones) moving across the sky.
