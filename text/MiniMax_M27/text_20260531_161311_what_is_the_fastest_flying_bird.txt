The **peregrine falcon** ( *Falco peregrinus* ) is widely recognized as the fastest flying bird. During its hunting dive—called a “stoop”—it can reach speeds of **240 – 250 mph (≈ 386 – 400 km/h)**, making it the quickest animal in level flight or diving. (For comparison, the common swift holds the record for the fastest sustained level flight, at about 70 mph/112 km/h.)
