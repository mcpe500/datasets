Here are three classic examples of things that can produce a spark:

1. **Lightning** – a natural electrical discharge in the atmosphere that creates a bright flash and a sudden spark.  
2. **A struck match** – friction between the match head and a rough surface generates enough heat to ignite the chemicals, producing a small flame and a spark.  
3. **A spark plug** – used in internal‑combustion engines, it delivers a high‑voltage electrical spark to ignite the fuel‑air mixture.
