Here are three classic examples of things that tend to keep getting better over time:

1. **Technology & Computing Power** – Processors, storage, algorithms, and software continuously become faster, more efficient, and more capable.  
2. **Medical Science & Healthcare** – Treatments, diagnostic tools, vaccines, and overall patient care improve as research, data, and clinical experience accumulate.  
3. **Renewable Energy & Energy‑Storage Technologies** – Solar panels, wind turbines, batteries, and grid‑management systems steadily rise in efficiency and drop in cost.
