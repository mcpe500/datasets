**Verse 1**  
The wind once whispered through a restless mind,  
Searching for a sign, a quiet clue,  
Each step a question left me far behind,  
Until a spark broke through the darkest hue.  

**Verse 2**  
A sunrise painted hope upon the sky,  
A voice inside insisted, “You belong,”  
It steadied doubts like birds that learn to fly,  
And planted purpose where I thought I’m wrong.  

**Verse 3**  
The path grew clear, the thorns turned into flowers,  
Each trial forged a strength I never knew,  
I gathered light from fleeting, fleeting hours,  
And turned the night into a canvas new.  

**Verse 4**  
Now every breath I breathe is born of meaning,  
My heart beats louder than the distant drums,  
The world expands beyond the walls of fearing,  
And purpose’s anthem rises, never numb.  

**Chorus**  
I found my purpose, shining bright as day,  
It steadies every step, it calms the sea,  
With fire in my soul, I light the way,  
And now I’m finally free.
