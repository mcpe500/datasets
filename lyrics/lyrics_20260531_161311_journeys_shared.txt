**Verse 1**  
We set out when the dawn first broke the night,  
With backpacks full of dreams and hopes alight.  
The road unspools, a ribbon through the hills,  
Our laughter rides the wind, our heartbeat thrills.  

**Verse 2**  
Miles stretch like verses in an endless song,  
Each turn a choice, each hill a chance to be strong.  
Through rain and shine we stumble and we soar,  
Our footprints mark the path we all adore.  

**Verse 3**  
We trade our stories where the campfire glows,  
And in each other's eyes the future flows.  
The quiet moments echo louder than the din,  
‘Cause every step we take, we’re walking kin.  

**Verse 4**  
Now twilight paints the sky in amber hues,  
Our journey’s tales are written in the blues.  
The road may fade, but memory stays bright,  
We’re forever bound by that shared starlight.  

**Chorus**  
We’re walking together, the world wide and wide,  
With every sunrise, we rise side by side.  
Hand in hand we chase the horizon’s flame,  
Our journeys shared, forever to remain.
