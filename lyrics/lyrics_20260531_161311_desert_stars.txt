**Verse 1**  
Beyond the dunes the night falls soft and wide,  
A tapestry of desert stars ignites the sky,  
Each glimmer sings of ancient, endless sighs,  
And the sand holds secrets in silver light.  

**Chorus**  
Desert stars, you’re the compass of my soul,  
Guiding my heart through the dunes of night,  
Your glow ignites the fire within my walk,  
Desert stars, forever burning bright.

**Verse 3**

**Verse 4**  
When morning’s ember kisses the sand,  
Your glow may fade, but it never dies,
