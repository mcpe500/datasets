Here are three common items you’ll find in most kitchens:

1. **Refrigerator** – a large appliance used to keep food and beverages cold.  
2. **Stove (or cooktop)** – the main cooking surface where you sauté, boil, or fry foods.  
3. **Sink** – a basin with a faucet used for washing dishes, hands, and food prep items.
