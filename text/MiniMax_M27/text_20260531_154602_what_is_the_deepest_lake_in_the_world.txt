The deepest lake in the world is **Lake Baikal** in Siberia, Russia.

- **Maximum depth:** ≈ 1,642 m (5,387 ft)  
- **Location:** Southern Siberia, within the Irkutsk Oblast and the Republic of Buryatia  
- **Notable facts:**  
  - It holds about 23 % of the world’s fresh surface water, making it the largest freshwater lake by volume.  
  - It is also the world’s oldest lake, with an estimated age of 25–30 million years.  
  - Its unique biodiversity includes many endemic species, such as the Baikal seal (nerpa) and the Baikal oil fish.  

**Runner‑up:** The second‑deepest lake is Lake Tanganyika in East Africa, with a maximum depth of about 1,470 m (4,823 ft).
