Here are four common forms of precipitation:

1. **Rain** – Liquid water droplets that fall from clouds when the temperature is above freezing.  
2. **Snow** – Ice crystals that form in the cold upper atmosphere and fall as flakes when the air temperature remains below freezing all the way to the ground.  
3. **Sleet (or ice pellets)** – Small, translucent balls of ice that form when rain passes through a shallow layer of freezing air near the surface.  
4. **Hail** – Solid blocks or lumps of ice that grow as super‑cooled water droplets freeze onto them inside strong thunderstorm clouds; they can range from tiny pellets to several centimeters in diameter.
