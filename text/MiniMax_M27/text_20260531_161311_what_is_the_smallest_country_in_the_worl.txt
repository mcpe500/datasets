The smallest country in the world is **Vatican City** (the Holy See).

- **Area:** about 0.44 km² (44 hectares) – the tiniest sovereign state by land area.  
- **Population:** roughly 800 people (as of recent estimates).  
- **Location:** an enclave within Rome, Italy.  
- **Status:** the headquarters of the Roman Catholic Church and a sovereign entity under international law.

(If you’re curious about the next‑smallest, Monaco, Nauru, and Tuvalu are also among the world’s tiniest states.)
