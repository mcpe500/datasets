**Verse 1**  
Golden sunrise paints the sand,  
Whispers of tides in fairy hands.  
Each shell a story, soft and bright,  
A melody that leads the night.  

**Verse 2**  
Sea‑foam glitters, dreams take flight,  
Mirrors of wonder in the light.  
Seagulls chant on winds of grace,  
Inviting hearts to find their place.  

**Verse 3**  
Moon‑lit coves where shadows dance,  
Secret smiles in sparkling trance.  
Starlit shells sing ancient lore,  
Calling wanderers to explore.  

**Verse 4**  
Morning dew on stones of gold,  
Echoes of the stories told.  
The horizon sings a sweet refrain,  
A promise that we’ll meet again.  

**Chorus**  
On the shores of wonder we roam,  
Where every wave becomes a home.  
Our souls are kissed by ocean’s song,  
In endless wonder we belong.
