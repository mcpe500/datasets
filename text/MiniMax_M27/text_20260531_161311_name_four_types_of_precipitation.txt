Here are four common forms of precipitation:

1. **Rain** – Liquid water droplets that fall from clouds when the temperature is above freezing throughout the lower atmosphere.
2. **Snow** – Ice crystals that form in cold clouds and remain frozen as they fall, accumulating as snowflakes when surface temperatures are at or below freezing.
3. **Sleet (or ice pellets)** – Small, translucent ice particles that form when snow melts partially while falling through a shallow layer of warm air and then refreezes before reaching the ground.
4. **Hail** – Layered ice pellets that grow by repeatedly ascending and descending within strong thunderstorm updrafts, becoming larger before finally falling to the ground.
