**Verse 1**  
In the early dawn we walked the same old street,  
Two silhouettes, a rhythm we’d repeat.  
Your laughter lit the corners of my mind,  
And every step we took, a story intertwined.  

**Verse 2**  
When stormy clouds gathered, we held tight,  
Your hand in mine, a compass through the night.  
Our thoughts would merge like rivers meeting sea,  
A melody of two, forever wild and free.  

**Verse 3**  
Through seasons turning, we spun webs of light,  
Your dreams and mine, a tapestry in flight.  
We traded songs of hope and whispered pain,  
Two lives that danced within a shared refrain.  

**Verse 4**  
Now shadows fade, the sunrise paints the sky,  
Our paths still ripple, though they weave and fly.  
The echo of our steps still rings in time,  
A harmony of lives, forever intertwined.  

**Chorus**  
We’re woven together, like threads in the wind,  
Two hearts beating in sync, never far, never pinned.  
In the tapestry of now, we’re the colors that blend,  
Bound by love and the promise that never will end.
