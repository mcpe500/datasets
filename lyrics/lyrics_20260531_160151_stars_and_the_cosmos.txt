**Verse 1**  
When twilight folds the world in velvet night,  
The heavens bloom with a million bright suns,  
Each star a whisper, a distant light,  
Calling the dreamers to chase what’s undone.  

**Verse 2**  
Nebulae paint the dark with violet haze,  
Galaxies spin like great silver wheels,  
Their ancient glow a timeless praise,  
A chorus of suns that forever reveals.  

**Verse 3**  
We trace the constellations, map their sighs,  
Orion’s belt, the Bear’s steady stride,  
Each point a beacon that never dies,  
Guiding the wanderer’s heart to its tide.  

**Verse 4**  
In the cold silence of the infinite void,  
Our pulse aligns with the pulse of the sky,  
We’re born of stardust, forever employed,  
In the cosmic dance, we lift our eyes high.  

**Chorus**  
Oh, we are stardust, glowing bright,  
Born from the fire of a distant star,  
In the endless night we find our light,  
Together we shine, forever and far.
