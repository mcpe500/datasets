The largest bird in the world today is the **common ostrich** (*Struthio camelus*). 

Here are some key facts about the ostrich:
- **Weight**: Males can weigh up to **156 kg (345 lbs)**, making them the heaviest living bird.
- **Height**: They can stand as tall as **2.8 meters (9.2 feet)**.
- **Speed**: While they cannot fly, they are the fastest running bird, reaching speeds up to 70 km/h (43 mph).

Ostriches are native to Africa and are well adapted to life in open savannas and semi-desert regions. They are primarily herbivores, eating roots, leaves, seeds, and some insects.

For comparison, the **largest flying bird** by wingspan is often considered the **wandering albatross** (*Diomedea exulans*), with wingspans reaching up to **3.5 meters (11.5 feet)**. The **Andean condor** and **great white pelican** are also among the largest flying birds.

**Fun historical note**: The extinct **elephant bird** (*Aepyornis*) of Madagascar was even heavier than the ostrich, with estimates suggesting it weighed up to **500 kg (1,100 lbs)**, though it is no longer alive today.
