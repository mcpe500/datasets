Here’s a quick reference with one onomatopoeic word (or sound‑effect) for each letter of the alphabet. The examples capture typical sounds you might hear in everyday life or in storytelling.

| Letter | Onomatopoeia | What it evokes |
|--------|--------------|----------------|
| **A** | *ahh* | a soft sigh of relief or surprise |
| **B** | *buzz* | the hum of a bee or a buzzing machine |
| **C** | *clang* | a loud, metallic strike (bell, hammer, etc.) |
| **D** | *ding* | a high, clear bell or chime |
| **E** | *eek* | a high‑pitched mouse or surprise squeak |
| **F** | *fizz* | the bubbles popping in a carbonated drink |
| **G** | *gurgle* | water bubbling as it pours or a baby’s burbling |
| **H** | *hiss* | the sharp sound of a snake, steam, or air escaping |
| **I** | *ick* | a disgusted “yuck!” reaction |
| **J** | *jingle* | the bright, light ringing of small bells or keys |
| **K** | *knock* | a sharp rap on a door or wood |
| **L** | *lap* | the rhythmic splashing of water (e.g., a dog drinking) |
| **M** | *meow* | the classic feline vocalization |
| **N** | *neigh* | the call of a horse |
