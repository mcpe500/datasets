In folklore the classic answer is a **pot of gold** guarded by a leprechaun. That’s the treasure that’s said to sit at the very end of a rainbow.

In the real world, however, a rainbow is an optical illusion created by sunlight refracting, reflecting, and dispersing inside countless water droplets. Because the light is spread out into the spectrum of colors you see, the “rainbow” is actually a circle of light that follows your line of sight—no fixed point you can walk to. So while the myth promises a pot of gold, the actual “end” of a rainbow isn’t a physical location you can reach.
