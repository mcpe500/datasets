Here are five common types of fruit:

1. **Apple** – crisp, sweet to tart, and great for eating fresh or in pies and sauces.  
2. **Banana** – a soft, creamy fruit that’s rich in potassium and perfect for smoothies or on its own.  
3. **Orange** – juicy and citrusy, often used for fresh juice, segments, or zest.  
4. **Mango** – a tropical stone fruit with sweet, fragrant flesh that’s delicious in salads, salsas, or smoothies.  
5. **Strawberry** – a red, berry‑like fruit with a juicy, slightly sweet flavor, often enjoyed fresh, in desserts, or as jam.
