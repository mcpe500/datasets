Here are three classic examples of things that tend to keep getting better over time:

1. **Technology** – From computing power to consumer gadgets, new designs, materials, and software iterations continually push performance higher and costs lower.  
2. **Scientific knowledge** – Ongoing research refines our understanding of nature, leading to better models, treatments, and innovations.  
3. **Renewable‑energy tech** – Solar panels, wind turbines, battery storage, and related systems steadily become more efficient, affordable, and widely adopted.
