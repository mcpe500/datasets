Here are three well‑known genres of music:

1. **Rock** – characterized by strong vocals, electric guitars, and a driving rhythm section.  
2. **Jazz** – known for improvisation, complex harmonies, and a blend of brass, woodwinds, and piano.  
3. **Classical** – the art music tradition rooted in Western notation, featuring orchestras, chamber ensembles, and forms such as symphonies and sonatas.
