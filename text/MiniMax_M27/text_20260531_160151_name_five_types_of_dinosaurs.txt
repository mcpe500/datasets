Here are five well‑known dinosaurs (their genus names):

1. **Tyrannosaurus** – The massive “king of the tyrant lizards,” famous for its powerful jaws and tiny fore‑arms.  
2. **Triceratops** – A large, horned herbivore with a bony frill around its neck.  
3. **Stegosaurus** – Recognizable by the row of bony plates along its back and the spikes on its tail.  
4. **Velociraptor** – A small, swift, feathered dromaeosaurid often portrayed as a cunning pack hunter.  
5. **Brachiosaurus** – A gigantic, long‑necked sauropod that grazed on high vegetation.
