Here are three common examples of things that glow in the dark:

1. **Glow‑in‑the‑dark stickers or toys** – often coated with phosphorescent pigments that absorb light and re‑emit it slowly.
2. **Fireflies (lightning bugs)** – bioluminescent insects that produce a flashing glow to attract mates.
3. **Phosphorescent paint or tape** – used on emergency exit signs, watch dials, and decorative items; it stores light energy and releases it as a visible glow after the lights go out.
