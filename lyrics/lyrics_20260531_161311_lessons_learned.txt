**Verse 1**  
I fell beneath the thunder, cold and bruised,  
But each scar wrote a story I refused to lose.  
I learned to listen when the silence called my name,  
And turn the bitter into fuel for brighter flame.  

**Verse 2**  
A shattered mirror showed a face I barely knew,  
I gathered up the pieces, saw a path break through.  
Trust is a seed that needs the rain of time to grow,  
And patience taught me how to let the weak winds blow.  

**Verse 3**  
I chased the lights that glittered far beyond my reach,  
Then stumbled, fell, and learned that longing has its speech.  
When pride slipped through my fingers like the finest sand,  
Humility rose steady, a steadier hand.  

**Verse 4**  
The road I walked once twisted, turned, and seemed so long,  
But every fork and detour taught me where I belong.  
I carry every lesson like a compass in my chest,  
Turning doubt to certainty, unrest to rest.  

**Chorus**  
Lessons learned, they echo in the quiet night,  
A whispered truth that turns the dark to light.  
With every step I take, I’m wiser, I’m refined—  
The scars become the verses of a brighter song.
