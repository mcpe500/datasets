The world’s tallest building is the **Burj Khalifa** in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. It stands **828 meters (2,717 feet)** tall and has 163 floors. It was completed in 2010 and holds the record for the tallest building (by a variety of measures) to date. 

*(While the Jeddah Tower in Saudi Arabia is intended to surpass it once finished—planned to reach over 1,000 m—it remains under construction and is not yet the tallest.)*
