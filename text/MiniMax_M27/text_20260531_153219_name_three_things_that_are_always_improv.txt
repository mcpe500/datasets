Here are three classic examples of areas that tend to keep getting better over time:

1. **Technology & Computing Power** – Processors, storage, software, and connectivity keep becoming faster, smaller, and more efficient.  
2. **Medical Science & Healthcare** – New drugs, diagnostic tools, surgical techniques, and preventive measures continually raise life expectancy and quality of care.  
3. **Renewable Energy & Energy‑Storage Technologies** – Solar panels, wind turbines, batteries, and grid‑management systems are steadily growing in efficiency and falling in cost.
