The **ostrich (Struthio camelus)** is the largest living bird on the planet today.

- **Weight:** up to about 156 kg (≈ 345 lb) for adult males.  
- **Height:** up to 2.8 m (≈ 9 ft) tall.  
- **Speed:** can sprint at up to 70 km/h (≈ 45 mph).

If you include extinct species, the **elephant birds** (Aepyornis) of Madagascar were heavier—estimated at 500–700 kg (≈ 1,100–1,500 lb)—and the teratorn **Argentavis magnificens** had the largest known wingspan (≈ 7 m, ≈ 23 ft). But among birds alive today, the ostrich holds the record for overall size.
