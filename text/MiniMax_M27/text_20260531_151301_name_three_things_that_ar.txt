Here are three areas that are constantly getting better:

- **Technology** – hardware, software, AI, and digital tools keep advancing, delivering faster, more capable, and more efficient solutions.  
- **Medical science** – new treatments, diagnostics, vaccines, and surgical techniques improve health outcomes and extend lifespans.  
- **Renewable energy** – solar, wind, battery storage, and related grid technologies steadily become more efficient, affordable, and widely adopted.
