**Verse 1**  
Beyond the sunrise, the world calls my name,  
A compass spins, a promise in the flame.  
Footsteps echo on the untrodden trail,  
Every sunrise whispers, “Set your sail.”

**Verse 2**  
Through emerald forests where the mosses sigh,  
I chase the rustle of wings that never lie.  
Hidden springs glisten, secrets untold,  
In the hush of the trees, I find my gold.

**Verse 3**  
Into the ocean’s deep, the tide’s a guide,  
Sunken ruins echo stories of the tide.  
Currents swirl like riddles, pulling me near,  
In the blue I uncover what I hold dear.

**Verse 4**  
Across the starlit plains where horizons blend,  
I trace the constellations, my heart’s best friend.  
Desert winds whisper maps of distant lands,  
Each step a new horizon, my soul expands.

**Chorus**  
Adventure’s fire, discovery’s light,  
We wander, we wonder, we rise, we take flight.  
With every new horizon, we’re alive,  
In the quest for the unknown, we thrive.
