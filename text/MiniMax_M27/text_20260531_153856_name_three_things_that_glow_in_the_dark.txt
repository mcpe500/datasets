Here are three things that glow in the dark:

1. **Fireflies** - These insects produce bioluminescent light through a chemical reaction in their bodies, typically to attract mates.

2. **Glow-in-the-dark stickers/paint** - These absorb light during the day and slowly release it as a soft glow in darkness.

3. **Certain mushrooms** - Some species, like *Mycena* mushrooms, produce a natural bioluminescent glow.
