Sure! Here are five examples of wild animals:

1. **Lion** – A large, social cat native to Africa and parts of India.  
2. **Elephant** – The world’s largest land mammal, known for its trunk and tusks.  
3. **Gray Wolf** – A highly social canid found across North America, Europe, and Asia.  
4. **Komodo Dragon** – A giant lizard from Indonesian islands, the world’s largest living species of lizard.  
5. **Red Fox** – A widespread omnivorous canid recognized by its reddish‑orange fur and bushy tail.
