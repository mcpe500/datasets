**Verse 1**  
Time’s a river that carries us away,  
but I’ve locked our moments in a quiet bay.  
Photographs and laughter echo in the night,  
preserved like amber, glowing soft and bright.  

**Chorus**

In every heart‑beat, a memory stays,  
a timeless song that never fades away.
