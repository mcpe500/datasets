**Verse 1**  
Morning glows, the city wakes in doubt,  
Silent pleas whisper through the crowds.  
Hands extended, a soft refrain of hope—  
Your fingers meet mine, a lifeline found.  

**Verse 2**  
Rain beats the streets, but kindness pours,  
Thunder can’t drown the voice within.  
Reach out, feel the tremor of another’s breath—  
Your grasp quiets the storm within.  

**Verse 3**  
Night whispers secrets, shadows linger,  
Loneliness lingers, yet love is near.  
Hands extended, the language of the soul—  
We speak through touch, no need for fear.  

**Verse 4**  
Dawn breaks, painting hope across the sky,  
Each open hand a brushstroke of light.  
Together we rise, in unity we stand—  
Our chorus sung in every heart’s fight.  

**Chorus**  
Hands extended, hearts beat as one,  
In every palm a story to unfold.  
Reach out, let love be the song—  
Together we rise, together we hold.
