**Verse 1**  
Old photograph frames on the mantle, dust in the light,  
A yellowed map of a town we used to call home.  
We’d chase fireflies in the dusk, laughing under the night,  
Now the echo of those footsteps feels forever on its own.  

**Verse 2**  
Summer’s lazy breeze still smells of fresh‑cut grass,  
The radio humming “our” song in the kitchen, a soft refrain.  
Ice‑cold lemonade on the porch, time slipping fast,  
Yet the taste of those days lingers, a warm refrain.  

**Verse 3**  
Friends that drifted like leaves, now miles away,  
Schoolyard whispers turning into silent sighs.  
We’d build forts from blankets, dreaming far away,  
Now the memory of that courage softly cries.  

**Verse 4**  
The old swing set creaks, a lullaby of the past,  
A lullaby that pulls me back to a simpler place.  
When sunrise painted the hills in amber cast,  
I hold each recollection close, a timeless trace.  

**Chorus**  
Oh, nostalgia, you’re a soft, endless tide,  
Bringing back the moments we thought we’d left behind.  
You paint the past in colors that never fade,  
And in every heartbeat, a song of what we find.
