**Verse 1**  
Morning light spills on the sand,  
A golden tide whispers your name,  
Palm trees sway to the salty breeze,  
Tropical shores, my heart at ease.  

**Verse 2**  
Coconut shells clink in the shade,  
Cool waves lap where the children played,  
Sun‑kissed skin, a rhythm of the drum,  
Every sunrise feels like home.  

**Verse 3**  
Lime‑green hills meet the endless blue,  
Hummingbirds dive in the humid hue,  
I chase the gulls across the pier,  
Dreams float on the trade‑wind clear.  

**Verse 4**  
Evening falls, the sky turns rose,  
Starlit surf in a gentle doze,  
The moonlit tide pulls us close,  
Together we dance on the shoreline’s throes.  

**Chorus**  
Tropical shores, you call me home,  
Where sapphire waves and bright breezes roam,  
In your warm embrace, I find my song,  
 forever drifting, never alone.
