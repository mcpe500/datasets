**Verse 1**  
Morning light spills soft on the ground,  
A giggle rises, a hush around.  
We chase the clouds, our hearts take flight,  
Every smile ignites the bright.

**Verse 2**  
Friends unite with a playful tune,  
Shared delight makes the night a moon.  
Silly jokes echo, echoing fun,  
Joyful beats under the endless sun.

**Verse 3**  
Kids run wild with laughter in tow,  
Echoes ripple, a gentle glow.  
Tears of mirth in the air we spin,  
Together we let the world begin.

**Verse 4**  
When darkness falls and worries loom,  
The sound of joy can chase the gloom.  
A heartfelt chuckle, a bright hello,  
Lights the night wherever we go.

**Chorus**  
Laughter and joy, the rhythm we share,  
A melody that lifts us everywhere.  
From sunrise till the stars shine clear,  
Our hearts beat loud with the sound of cheer.
