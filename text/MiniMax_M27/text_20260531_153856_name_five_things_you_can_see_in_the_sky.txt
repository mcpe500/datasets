Here are five common things you can see when you look up at the sky:

1. **Clouds** – Fluffy, puffy, or wispy formations of water vapor floating above us.
2. **The Sun** – Our star, shining brightly during the day.
3. **The Moon** – The Earth’s natural satellite, often visible especially at night.
4. **Stars** – Tiny points of light that appear when the sky is dark, part of our Milky Way galaxy.
5. **Airplanes** – Aircraft leaving contrails as they travel across the atmosphere.
