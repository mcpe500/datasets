The tallest living animal on Earth is the **giraffe**.  

- **Height:** Adult male giraffes (the taller sex) can reach up to about 5.5 m (18 ft) from ground to the top of the head, and their neck alone can be around 2 m (6.5 ft) long.  
- **Species:** The most common species is the **reticulated giraffe** (*Giraffa camelopardalis reticulata*), but all giraffe subspecies share roughly the same stature.  
- **Record‑holders:** The tallest confirmed individual on record was a male named **George**, who measured 5.88 m (19 ft 3 in) tall.  

So, when it comes to the tallest animal alive today, the giraffe holds the title. (If you’re counting extinct species, some long‑necked sauropod dinosaurs (e.g., *Sauroposeidon*) were taller, but among living animals the giraffe is the champion.)
