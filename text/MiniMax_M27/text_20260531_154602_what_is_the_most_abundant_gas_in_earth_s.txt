The most abundant gas in Earth’s atmosphere is **nitrogen (N₂)**, making up about **78 %** of the dry air by volume.  

For reference, the major components of the atmosphere are roughly:

| Gas | Approximate volume fraction |
|-----|-----------------------------|
| Nitrogen (N₂) | 78.08 % |
| Oxygen (O₂)   | 20.95 % |
| Argon (Ar)    | 0.93 % |
| Carbon dioxide (CO₂) | ~0.04 % (and rising) |
| Neon, helium, methane, etc. | trace amounts |

So, if you look at the composition of the air you breathe, nitrogen dominates the mix.
