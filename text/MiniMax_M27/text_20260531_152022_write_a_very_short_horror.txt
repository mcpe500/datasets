She found the mirror in the attic, its glass clouded with age. When she wiped the dust, she saw herself—eyes wide, mouth half‑open. She looked away, but the reflection kept staring. She turned around; no one was behind her. Then the glass began to whisper, “You’re still here.” A chill crawled up her spine, and the room grew colder. She tried to leave, but each step echoed back as if she were walking in place. The mirror cracked, and from the fracture poured a soft, suffocating sigh, as if something had finally escaped. The attic door slammed shut, sealing her in.
