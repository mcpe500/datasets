The corridor stretched endless, the floorboards whispering each step. Sarah heard a faint breath behind her, cold, rattling. She turned—nothing. Then, from the darkness of the doorway, a child's voice whispered, “Don’t look back.” Her chest tightened as the whisper grew louder, closing in, and the walls began to pulse. The last thing she felt was the tickle of fingers brushing her neck. She never turned around.
