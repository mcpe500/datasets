Here are four common types of birds:

1. **Eagle** – a large, powerful bird of prey known for its keen eyesight and soaring flight.  
2. **Sparrow** – a small, often brownish songbird that is common in urban and rural areas worldwide.  
3. **Penguin** – a flightless seabird adapted to life in cold, southern waters, famous for its black‑and‑white plumage.  
4. **Ostrich** – the world’s largest bird, also flightless, native to the open savannas of Africa.
