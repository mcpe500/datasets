**Five benefits of meditation**

1. **Reduces stress** – Regular meditation lowers cortisol levels and helps the body relax, making everyday pressures feel more manageable.  
2. **Improves focus and concentration** – By training the mind to stay present, meditation strengthens attention span and mental clarity.  
3. **Enhances emotional well‑being** – Practices such as mindfulness can increase positive emotions, reduce anxiety, and foster a more balanced mood.  
4. **Promotes better sleep** – Calming the mind before bedtime helps transition into deeper, more restorative sleep patterns.  
5. **Supports physical health** – Meditation has been linked to lower blood pressure, improved immune function, and reduced inflammation.
