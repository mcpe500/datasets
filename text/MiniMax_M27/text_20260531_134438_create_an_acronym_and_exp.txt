# ACRONYM: **R.I.S.E.**

**R** - **R**eflect on your goals  
**I** - **I**dentify obstacles  
**S** - **S**eek support and resources  
**E** - **E**xecute with consistency  

**Meaning:** This acronym reminds us that personal growth and achieving goals follow a four-step process. First, you take time to understand what you truly want. Second, you honestly recognize what's standing in your way. Third, you gather the tools and people that can help you. Finally, you put in the daily work needed to move forward.

It serves as a simple mental checklist whenever someone feels stuck or overwhelmed and needs a quick framework to get back on track.
