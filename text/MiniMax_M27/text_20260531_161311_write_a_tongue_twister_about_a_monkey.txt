**Slick‑sly, slippery Sam the monkey**  
swings swift up the sycamore’s slime‑slicked stem.  

**Six sleek monkeys**  
swing, spin, and spin again, slipping, sliding, and singing “Shush!”  

**Mischief‑making, mango‑munching, mischief‑making**  
makes the monkeys’ mouths merry, messy, and mad.  

**Sammy the sly simian**  
sips sweet, syrupy soda, then slaps his sibling, and the silly syllables slip, slap, and skip.  

*(Say it three times fast – if you stumble, the monkeys tumble!)*
