Here’s a quick list of one common onomatopoeia for each letter of the alphabet. (Some letters have fewer obvious options, so a few are creative / comic‑book‑style sound effects.)

| Letter | Onomatopoeia | Typical context |
|--------|--------------|-----------------|
| **A** | **Achoo** | a sneeze |
| **B** | **Buzz** | a bee or insect |
| **C** | **Click** | a camera shutter, a latch |
| **D** | **Drip** | water falling drop by drop |
| **E** | **Eek** | a mouse or sudden fright |
| **F** | **Fizz** | soda or carbonated drink |
| **G** | **Gurgle** | water moving in a pipe or a baby’s stomach |
| **H** | **Hiss** | a snake or steam escaping |
| **I** | **Ick** | a reaction of disgust |
| **J** | **Jingle** | a small bell or keys |
| **K** | **Knock** | a knock on a door |
| **L** | **Lap** | water splashing against the shore |
| **M** | **Moo** | a cow’s low‑pitch call |
| **N** | **Neigh** | a horse’s whinny |
| **O** | **Oink** | a pig’s grunt |
| **P** | **Pop** | a bubble bursting or a cork popping |
| **Q** | **Quack** | a duck’s call |
| **R** | **Ribbit** | a frog’s croak |
| **S** | **Splash** | water hitting a surface |
| **T** | **Tick‑tock** | the steady sound of a clock |
| **U** | **Ugh** | a groan or expression of disgust |
| **V** | **Vroom** | a car engine revving |
| **W** | **Wham** | a sudden, forceful impact |
| **X** | **X‑X** (often used in comics for a sharp crack or zap) | a sudden, electric‑like snap |
| **Y** | **Yip** | a small dog’s bark or a quick yelp |
| **Z** | **Zap** | an electric shock or laser shot |

Feel free to swap in any other onomatopoeic words you prefer; the table just gives a handy starting point!
