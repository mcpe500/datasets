**Burj Khalifa (Dubai, United Arab Emirates)**  

- **Height:** 828 m (2,717 ft) – the architectural height measured to the top of the spire.  
- **Floors:** 163 (plus 46 maintenance/staff levels).  
- **Completed:** 2010.  
- **Architect/Engineer:** Designed by Adrian Smith of Skidmore, Owings & Merrill; structural engineering by Hyder Consulting.  
- **Key records:**  
  - Tallest building in the world (since 2010).  
  - Tallest freestanding structure.  
  - Highest number of floors.  
  - Fastest construction (about 1.4 m per day).  

**Why it’s still the tallest**  
Even though several “supertall” projects have been announced—most notably the **Jeddah Tower (Kingdom Tower)** in Saudi Arabia, which is planned to exceed 1,000 m—none have been completed as of early 2026. The Burj Khalifa retains its title as the world’s tallest building.
