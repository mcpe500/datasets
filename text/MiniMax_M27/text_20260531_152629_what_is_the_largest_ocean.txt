The **Pacific Ocean** is the largest ocean on Earth.  

- **Area:** about 63 million sq mi (≈165 million km²) – roughly equal to the total land area of all the continents combined.  
- **Volume:** roughly 660 million km³ of water.  
- **Depth:** average depth about 4,000 m (13,000 ft); the deepest point, the Challenger Deep in the Mariana Trench, is about 10,935 m (35,876 ft) below sea level.  

It stretches from the western coast of the Americas to the eastern coast of Asia and Australia, and from the Arctic Ocean in the north to the Southern Ocean in the south.
